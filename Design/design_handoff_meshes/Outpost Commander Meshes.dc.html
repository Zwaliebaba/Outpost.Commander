MINE — DECIDED HERE

  • Hull palette — three tones, chroma under 0.018
  • Vertex-colour convention: R = team selector, G = hull tone
  • Triangle counts 126–500 — no budget; spend on section, hold the plan outline

  • Four distinct module meshes plus the bare frame
  • Five asteroid variants; jitter 0.75–1.35 scale, ±240 u off-plane
  • Tactical plates rendered at 85° pitch (the brief says only "near top-down")
FLAGGED — BOTH LEFT STANDING
  • Combat distance. "~1,400 units" cannot produce "1,638 wide / 1.14 u/px / 53 and 79 px" at vfov 40°. Those need d = 1,500. The 7% gap is unresolved; plates use 1,500 and say so.
  • The reference frame. The HUD handoff's 34° at focus distance 2,600 gives 1.97 u/px — a 60-unit hull at 30 px, not 53. The brief calls both a combat plate.
  • Asteroid extents. The seeded variants land at 55–167 u longest, not exactly 60–180. Jitter covers the range; the figures are not reconciled to the table.
  • The tree still states the old rule. ADR-005, TechnicalDesign §7, GameDesign §10, Design/README, M1.9, M2.4, M2.10b. Not touched here.
WHAT STILL NEEDS A RULING
  • Q37, hull sizes. Taken as recommended, unmoved. The 220-unit station is the packing constraint, not a free choice — a 300-unit station breaks the 400-unit module radius.
  • The overlay. Plate 7 is a readout that is not on the MVP list. Asked for and approved; it needs a question-register entry and a HUD-handoff amendment before anybody builds it.
  • Cruiser. Not authored, per the brief. Cost of authoring it later is in the README — it is one more file and one more draw call, but the shape language only has one axis left.
  • The CMO reader and the asset step. Both costs stand. The integration package at integration/ ships a convert-ready OBJ set, an asset manifest, UWP content declarations for Assets\Meshes\, and INTEGRATION_PROMPT.md. Two figures still need measuring there, not estimating: the appx size delta (the 188 KB of zeros compresses away) and the VRAM vertex-buffer size (it does not).
  • The handedness flag. Whether meshconvert needs a flip for this input is unresolved. An earlier draft asserted it did not; that is withdrawn and replaced by four landmark tests that name the axis when they fail.
