[R5-1] [Reviewer 5 Feasibility] [Major] [Feasibility]
Claim: M3.8's "the host reseeds and runs on" cannot be built on the current wire — the only reseed a connected client can detect is a one-second silence, and that path reassigns seats, never recenters, and has no result to show.
Evidence: FACT `Host.cpp:32-58` BeginMatch resets world/intake/build/economy, calls `m_sessions.Begin` (`Sessions.cpp:8-14`, every seat cleared) and `m_accumulator.Begin()` (`Accumulator.cpp:79-82`, every client forgotten), and never touches `m_tick` (`Host.h:210`). FACT ADR-013:151-155 names the gap and proposes "the tick going backwards" as the detector. FACT `ReplicaStore.cpp:27-32,54-58` refuse any record or own block whose tick is not newer. FACT `ClientFrame.h:81` / `ClientFrame.cpp:20-31` rejoin after 1,000 ms; `Sessions.cpp:66-119` answers a token no session holds with the lowest free slot; `App.cpp:643-659` recenters once (`reportedSeat`). FACT `Panels.h:86-88` `winner` "Nothing sets it before M3"; no header in GameCore/GameLogic names a phase or winner.
Scenario / proof: (a) As built: BeginMatch at victory → both clients silent 1,000 ms → each rejoins with a token the host no longer holds → whoever's Join lands first is player 1 (two clients retrying every 250 ms is a race) → the field is re-derived and re-baked correctly (`FieldView.cpp:17-28`, `App.cpp:1118`) but the camera stays on the old base, the selection keeps stale identities until the next tap, and the player reads "RECONNECTING / THE MATCH DID NOT WAIT" instead of a result. (b) If M3.8 keeps sessions to avoid (a): nothing on an update carries the seed, `m_tick` keeps counting so ADR-013's detector never fires, and if `m_tick` were reset to make it fire the client would refuse every record of the new match as "not newer" (`ReplicaStore.cpp:54`) until the sweep forgot them, while still drawing the old asteroid field.
Why it matters: M3.11 is twenty restarts in an evening; each costs a second of overlay, a possible human/AI seat swap, and no winner shown — the loop the reduced MVP exists for.
Recommendation: add two bytes to the update header — `matchGeneration` (u8, incremented per BeginMatch) and `winner` (PlayerId, NO_PLAYER while running): header 21→23, records/update stay 100 (1,209/12), floor stays 65 ((1232−23−144−280)/12), sweep unchanged; BeginMatch keeps `m_sessions` and only resets the accumulator's tracking; a client seeing a new generation shows the result overlay, then re-sends Join with its token (Rejoined → same slot, new seed → FieldView re-derives), clears selection/markers/arming and recenters; never reset `m_tick`. Protocol 6, ~1–2 days with tests. Alternative: a `MatchEnded` packet (winner, seed, count) repeated ten ticks like a removal — no header cost, a third record to version.
Confidence: High on what the code does; Medium on the two-byte layout being the cheapest of the two.

[R5-3] [Reviewer 5 Feasibility] [Major] [Feasibility]
Claim: The F3/ADR-014 cadence choice is bounded by the wire: with 40 fire events per update, three repeats each and only the first update of a tick carrying them, any cadence below ⌈3·mounts/40⌉ ticks backs up without bound — "fire every tick" is infeasible above 13 mounts (six fighters) and a 50-fighter engagement needs ≥8 ticks.

Confidence: High on the arithmetic and measurement; Medium on which mitigation the design prefers.

**Checked and found sound**
- R19's boundary is real, not a convention: `OutpostCommander` and `GameClient` link no `GameLogic`; the placement preview (`CheckModuleSite` at wire precision, inexactness documented in `ModuleSite.h:22-24`), the split-for-mine (`Derive().oreCapacity > 0`, mirrored by the intake), costs and the field all come from `GameCore`, and no simulation rule is duplicated on the client.
- The tick model: one thread, socket drained at the top, `TickSchedule` non-drifting with a ten-tick catch-up cap, and no clock anywhere in `GameLogic`; the simulation costs 13 µs / 25 µs per tick at 110 / 220 entities.
- Targeting on the uniform grid with identity-sorted candidates is the right shape for M3: measured 11 µs/tick spread and 104 µs in a 100-ship brawl at the MVP, 437 µs at 220 in a brawl, 6.7 ms at 5,500 — protect the "every query leaves sorted by identity" rule.
- The accumulator's sweep guarantee and deterministic sent set; R5-5's fix preserves both (0 of 32,000 records out of order).
- Free list from the back with the generation advanced at `Destroy`, 8-bit wire generation, removals detected "by looking" — M3's death system need not report anything.
- Command validation: signed 16-bit sequence wrap, ownership/bound/generation checks, oldest-first fill, the host resolving the sender from its session.
- The generator: integer-only, its own PRNG stream, exact quarter-turn copies; the field cross-check in R5-6 is insurance, not a repair.
- The baked asteroid field's 552 KiB upload heap is ~33 MB/s at 60 Hz (INFERENCE, arithmetic) — not a frame-time risk; the interface is already one instanced draw.
- Fixed-point movement with rounded division and arrive-and-snap, and the interpolation store's per-entity tick ordering, hold up for M3's removals and holds.
