Some states differ from a drawn one only in text or color, so they have no file of their own. Each is described in its section below.

  • Selection panel: a remembered structure, a Mining Rig, more than 24 ships, and SAVE against RENAME against UPDATE.
  • Queues: a queue waiting for the fleet cap, and a full queue.
  • Status panel: a Lab with every open topic done, and the Shipyards at the fleet cap.
  • Banners: Defeat, Draw, "A fleet destroyed." and "Out of time.".
