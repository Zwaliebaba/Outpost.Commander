Hardpoints

A hardpoint is a glTF empty named hp_<tag>. The game knows two tags: gun, where a shot leaves from, and exhaust (ADR-018 §Decision 4; Hardpoints.h:5-16; Hardpoints.cpp:9-10,45-50).

Hardpoints are presentation only. A shot leaves from the gun nearest its target, and gun n of a Command Station from the n-th nearest, wrapping round. Range is still measured from the ship's center (Hardpoints.h:68-73; Hardpoints.cpp:205-220).

The table counts the hardpoints in the glTF sources (count_hardpoints.py over Art/Models/<Set>/*.glb). The counts were checked against the baked .nmf files at OutpostCommander/Assets/Models/<Set>/*.nmf, by counting tag bytes. Human and Tarkan are identical.
