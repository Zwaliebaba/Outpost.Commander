Renderer

Renderer: a software rasterizer in Python 3.13 and numpy 2.5 (DesignReference/_work/A6/tool/nmfrender.py, driven by render_all.py), not three.js: z-buffered triangles, then a depth-tested line pass, then the glow, all in linear color, encoded to sRGB at the end. No Blender, no GPU. It is the same arithmetic as the shaders, replayed per pixel.

Culling Back faces culled, clockwise front (ADR-011 decision 7) A face is drawn when its normal faces the eye; every face's winding agrees with its normal (checked here on all 16,316 triangles, as GameAppTests holds).
Antialiasing 4× MSAA, averaged in linear color (ADR-040 decision 3, ADR-050) 2×2 supersampling averaged in linear color, plus analytic coverage on the lines.
Color encoding Linear colors into an R8G8B8A8_UNORM_SRGB target (ADR-040 decision 3) sRGB-encoded at the end.
Glow (ADR-091) SCENE_BLOOM threshold 0.35, knee 0.15, intensity 2, spread 0.6 (GameClient.cpp:25); five half-size levels: bright pass (BloomBrightPS.hlsl), 13-tap down (BloomDownPS.hlsl), 3×3 tent up weighted by the spread (BloomUpPS.hlsl), added in the resolve × intensity / Σspreadᵏ (ResolvePS.hlsl, Bloom.cpp:86-98) Added, as a light phosphor glow: the same passes and numbers replayed with bilinear taps on the 1024-pixel image. In the transparent PNGs it is kept over the model only (an added light cannot be stored in a transparent pixel without a pale halo on a light page); on the sheets, over black, it is added whole.
Camera Default yaw looks along +z, screen right +x, top +z (GameApp/Camera.cpp:13-14); pitch from 40° to 70° with zoom (Camera.json, Camera.cpp:160-165); 45° vertical field of view 3/4 view: the default yaw at the default zoom's pitch, 43.68° (view width 500 m of 150–3000 m, Camera.json), model heading 225° (nose toward the lower left). Top view: straight down, screen top +z, heading 90° (nose up). Orthographic: at the game's distance a model spans a few degrees, so this stands in for its perspective. Heading as WorldMatrix/PoseMatrix turn it (XMMatrixRotationY(−heading), GameClient.cpp:283-291, Hardpoints.cpp:54-64).

Where this is not the game, on purpose or of necessity:

  • Lines are 2 px wide at 1024 px (about 1 px in the _512 copies). The game draws 1-pixel lines at any zoom (ADR-027 decision 3), but a model fills ~880 px here against ~80 px for a 20 m hull at the default zoom (500 m across 1920 px), so 1 px would read far thinner than the game does.
  • Each image is framed by its own projected extent (the model fills 84 % of the side), so images are not to scale with each other. The level sheets are the exception: one frame for all five levels.
  • Not drawn: the starfield and grid behind the model, banking, exhaust glows at exhaust hardpoints (ADR-019), footprint rings (ADR-042), selection, health bars, a structure under construction (darker by UNBUILT_SHADE), a Mining Rig standing on its rock (ADR-044), a remembered structure's lines-only look (MEMORY_GRAY_SHARE, MEMORY_SHADE, GameClient.cpp:119-120). Spinning parts are drawn at rest (angle 0), as the baker and the explosion take them (ADR-045 decisions 2 and 5).
  • Not built, so not drawn (ImplementationPlan-Look.md task board): paint slots, two-tone lines, beacons, fleet plates (LV2.2, LV2.3, LV3.1, LV4.1–4.3).
  • Transparent background (straight alpha, written so that it shows as rendered over black in an ordinary viewer): the sky is not there, and a model's dark faces read lighter on a white page than on the game's black.
