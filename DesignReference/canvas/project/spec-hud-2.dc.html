Selection panel

Purpose. What is selected: its name, its state and its hit points, as figures and bars (ADR-043 d5, ADR-046 d6, ADR-066 d4, ADR-088 d4).

Geometry (Hud.cpp:3357-3426). - Width. w = clamp(the widest line + 24, 280, 560). Line 0 is measured in the Title face and the others in the Name face; the width is ≈ wherever it falls between the bounds. - Height. h = 24 + 30 + 22·(lines − 1) + bars. Bars add 8 + rows·10 + (rows − 1)·4 for per-ship bars, or 8 + 6 for the one bar. - Position. pairLeft = (1920 − w − buttonsPanelWidth)/2, and at least 324 when there is a minimap (Hud.cpp:3369-3371); y = 1064 − h. - Line 0 is in the Title face, TEXT_COLOR, at (12, 12). Lines 1 and on are in the Name face, NUMBERS_COLOR, at (12, 42 + 22·(i − 1)). Every line is cut short at w − 24. - Per-ship bars (2 to 24 ships, Hud.h:566): each 17×10, in rows of 12, 4 apart, starting 8 below the lines. - Track BAR_TRACK_COLOR. The fill runs from the left by the ship's share of its hit points: HEALTH_GOOD_COLOR above 0.5, HEALTH_FAIR_COLOR above 0.25, otherwise HEALTH_POOR_COLOR (Hud.cpp:1026-1029). - A 1-unit outline round a bar: TEXT_COLOR for a retreating ship, HOVER_EDGE_COLOR under the pointer. - A click on a bar selects that ship alone and centres the camera on it (Select, Hud.cpp:3399-3417; GameClient.cpp:957-963). - One bar otherwise: 12, top, w − 24, 6, in the same colors (Hud.cpp:3418-3425).

Line content (Hud::Describe, Hud.cpp:2559-2790, and PendingOrderLine added by GameClient.cpp:769-771).

Minimap

Purpose. The whole map: whose sectors are whose, the marks, the fog, the alerts, and the camera's view (ADR-015 d4, ADR-043 d7, ADR-068 d2, ADR-081).

Geometry (Hud.cpp:3456-3465). - The frame is 16, 764, 300, 300; the map area, MAP_COLOR, is 24, 772, 284, 284. - Scale. 284 units for Snapshot::mapSizeMeters, 10,000 m with Map.json sizeMeters: 0.0284 units a metre, or 35.2 m a unit. - Orientation. +x runs right and +z up (Hud.cpp:2432-2451).

Draw order and looks (all in layer 0's panels; the view's segments are lines). 1. Sector wash. Every held or pirate-guarded sector, filled in OWN_COLOR, ENEMY_COLOR or PIRATE_COLOR at alpha 0.12 (Hud.cpp:3481-3485, 42). A sector is 56.8 units square on Map.json's 5×5 grid of 2,000 m sectors. 2. Marks. The neutral ones are drawn first, then the sided ones (Hud.cpp:3486-3528). A mark is a square of side max(least, 2·radius·0.0284). The least sides are: a ship 3, an ore asteroid 8, anything else 6 (Hud.cpp:118-120). The map's asteroids and fields are always seen (Simulation.cpp:245-247), so every frame in a match has all 80 fields and all 40 ore asteroids: Map.json's 6 home ones and the 34 the seed places (Map.cpp:381-419), under the fog. An asteroid is dry only once the player knows its reserve is 0, which it learns in sight or in a sector it holds (Simulation.cpp:331-342). A derelict is seen as an enemy structure is, in sight or in a held, unsuppressed sector (Simulation.cpp:289-304, 2981-2987), and remembered after.

  1. Fog. A panel over the whole map area, Fill::Fog: black, as opaque as the fog texture's shade at each point (Hud.cpp:3531-3535; drawn GameClient.cpp:1310-1316; ADR-052). Only under fog of war.
  2. Lattice. Every sector's border, a 1-unit outline in LATTICE_COLOR (Hud.cpp:3567-3568).
  3. Held and guarded outlines. A 1-unit outline in the side's color at alpha 0.55. It is dashed (6 on, 4 off) while the sector is cut off; while it is suppressed, a hatched fill at alpha 0.35 covers the sector (Hud.cpp:3569-3580).
  4. Claimable sectors. While a Relay is placed, each sector the player could claim now is outlined in OWN_COLOR, 3 units inside its border (Hud.cpp:3584-3592).
  5. Remembered structures and derelicts. A cross 8 units across, with arms 2 thick, in the side's color, or DERELICT_COLOR for a neutral one (Hud.cpp:3595-3605).
  6. Alerts. A 14-unit square outlined 1 unit in WARNING_COLOR (Hud.cpp:3608-3617).
  7. The camera's view. The four ground corners of the screen, joined by segments 1.5 units wide in VIEW_COLOR, cut at the minimap's edge (Hud.cpp:3621-3629; GameClient.cpp:1040-1054). It is empty when a corner sees past the horizon. - At the default 500 m view, unturned, the derived trapezoid is ≈25 units across its far edge, ≈10 across its near edge and ≈14 deep (from Camera.cpp:160-236 and Camera.json).

Interactions. - A left press on the map area moves the camera there, and dragging keeps moving it (GameClient.cpp:1011-1015, 1026-1031). - A right press sends the selected ships there (GameClient.cpp:1017-1022). - The minimap draws only when mapSizeMeters > 0 (Hud.h:611-612).

Bars over entities

ADR-047 d4 and ADR-088 d5.

Geometry (Hud.cpp:3095-3126, 98-101). - Size. Each bar is 32×5 at the back buffer's scale, not the player's factor. Position and size are whole pixels. - Stacking. The health bar stands over the build bar, 2 apart, and the pair stands 4 above the "foot". The foot is the top of the model on the screen, in the entity's column (Camera::PixelAbove, GameClient.cpp:731-737). - Back: the side's color × 0.3 (HEALTH_BACK_SHADE, GameClient.cpp:93-98, 727), or HEALTH_BACK_COLOR for a side the data does not name. - Health fill: HEALTH_GOOD_COLOR, HEALTH_FAIR_COLOR or HEALTH_POOR_COLOR. A site starts at a tenth of its hit points and gains the rest with the work (Simulation.cpp:71, 2449-2456), so unless it is repaired a structure 40% built is at 0.1 + 0.9 × 0.4 = 46%, in HEALTH_FAIR_COLOR. - Build fill: BUILD_BAR_COLOR.

When a bar shows (Hud::BarOver, Hud.cpp:2170-2183). - The health bar shows while the entity is hurt, or for every entity while Alt is held (KeyBindings.h:38; GameClient.cpp:598). - The build bar shows while the structure is unfinished. - Nothing shows for what is only remembered. - A pair wholly off the screen is left out.

The bars take no click. Example pairs are in hud-busy-warships.json.
