Scope and sources

In scope

The MVP design as amended by Phases 1 → 5, the two interface plans and the built tasks of the Look plan (LV1.1, LV1.2, LV2.1), and the code and data at HEAD.

Out of scope

Horizon (design and plan), self-play (plan, network blueprint, ADR-063), and what a plan lists as not built: paint slots, two-tone lines, beacons, fleet plates (Look plan LV2.2–LV4.3).

Precedence

For what the player sees and the numbers behind it, code and data are ground truth; ADRs record why and usually the exact values; a later phase document wins over an earlier one; plans are work queues, not authorities (AGENTS.md). Where they disagree, a page shows the code's value and the disagreement is listed in 17 Open items.

How this was made

Seven extraction agents read the docs, ADRs, code and data; a reconciliation pass listed every conflict and gap; this canvas is generated from their files (DesignReference/_work/, DesignReference/compose/). Nothing was built or run: the container had no Windows, MSBuild or GPU. Screen geometry is derived from Hud.cpp's arithmetic, and model images are approximations rendered from the shipped meshes.
