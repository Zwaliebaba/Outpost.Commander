Match loop · player level
1Setup
The map's content is placed from the match's seed: asteroids, then pirate outposts, then derelicts, mirrored through the center. InProcessServer.cpp:133; Map.cpp:354-508; ADR-072 §Decision 3.
2Players added
with 1,000 Ore and the four starting designs saved; fog on; pirates and derelicts spawned. InProcessServer.cpp:150-167; Tuning.json: rules.startingOre, startingDesigns.
3Bases placed
each player's Command Station on its start (holding its home sector from the first tick) and 2 Constructors; 1,000 tickets each. Simulation.cpp:688-727; ADR-056 §Decision 3; ADR-057 §Decision 4.
4Mine
Constructors build Mining Rigs (50 Ore) on the three home asteroids; income flows each tick. Tuning.json line 60; Simulation.cpp:3003-3027; Phase2.md:69.
5Claim
Relays (200 Ore) on the two flank nodes open the near asteroids; further claims grow along the lattice, up to the Command Station's node cap. Phase2.md:53, Phase2.md:69; Simulation.cpp:2134-2140, 2170-2171.
6Build the base
Shipyard (in a held sector), Research Lab (one), Defence Platforms, Repair Bay; costs paid at the start, nothing refunded. Simulation.cpp:2145-2185; MVP.md:150.
7Explore and salvage
scouts find derelicts and pirate outposts; Constructors salvage derelicts for Ore and research; fleets clear outposts to open guarded sectors. Phase4.md:21; ADR-074 §Decision 4-5; ADR-073 §Decision 6.
8Research and grow
research topics within the Lab's open tier; upgrade the Command Station (nodes, fleet cap, guns), the Shipyard (hulls) and the Lab (tiers, second slot). ADR-064 §Decision 9-11; Tuning.json lines 40-58.
9Design and produce
warships from saved designs, each job waiting for Ore and for room under the fleet cap. Simulation.cpp:2674-2762; ADR-071 §Decision 3.
10Fight
suppress and destroy enemy Relays, defend held sectors; damaged ships retreat at their threshold and are repaired at a Repair Bay, the Command Station or a Shipyard. ADR-056 §Decision 6; ADR-075 §Decision 5.
11Ore runs out
home rigs run dry and pay the 20% trickle; income moves outward. ADR-035 §Decision 2; Phase1.md:206.
12Domination drains
every 3 s by the node difference; a player out of tickets loses. Simulation.cpp:3778-3805.
13Losing production
(no Command Station and no finished Shipyard) loses the match; a station-less player's Shipyards are revealed. Simulation.cpp:3683-3710, 316-330.
14End
Victory, Defeat or Draw banner with the match's length ("By domination." when so); the world runs on until the player goes back to the menu. Hud.cpp:2199-2207; ADR-020 §Decision 9.

World loop · a persistent world on a dedicated server
1Make a world
OutpostServer --new-world <folder> makes one with a seed and the seats; OutpostServer <folder> runs it. A new world gets the world's rules (no domination, restarts). Phase5.md:64; InProcessServer.cpp:86-90.
2Recover on start
load the newest save that reads whole, then apply the logged commands since it, tick by tick. Phase5.md:84; WorldFolder.cpp:104-130; InProcessServer.cpp:78-83.
3Tick
20 a second; each tick's commands are written to the world's command log before they are applied. Tuning.json: rules.tickHz; InProcessServer.cpp:477-482; Phase5.md:83.
4Save
every 60 s of ticks; the newest 3 saves kept; written to a new file and renamed into place. WorldFolder.h:15 (WORLD_SAVE_SECONDS = 60), WorldFolder.h:23 (SAVES_KEPT = 3); InProcessServer.cpp:484-485; Phase5.md:82.
5Seats
a seat nobody has taken yet is its deputy's; a player takes its seat with its token and plays as in a match. SeatController.cpp:9-12; Phase5.md:67, Phase5.md:95.
6Away
60 s after the player's connection drops, the deputy (a keeper) takes the seat; scheduled orders fire on their ticks. GameProtocol/Server.h:32 (DEPUTY_DELAY_SECONDS = 60); SeatController.cpp:13-21; Phase5.md:47, Phase5.md:95-96, Phase5.md:107.
7Return
the player takes the seat again; the deputy gives it back; a panel says what happened while it was away; what the deputy ordered stands. Phase5.md:95, Phase5.md:155; InProcessServer.cpp:521-531.
8Loss and restart
a player without a Command Station or a finished Shipyard is told; an hour of ticks later, once its start is free, it restarts with a Command Station, 2 Constructors and its Ore topped up to 1,000. Simulation.cpp:3715-3748; Simulation.h:343.
9Hourly record
the world log writes each seat's Ore, income, fleet against its cap, nodes, topics researched, and time present and deputy-played, every hour of ticks. WorldLog.cpp:101-115; Phase5.md:137, Phase5.md:143.
10Outage
while the server is down the world pauses; on start it recovers (step 2). Phase5.md:85.
11End
a world ends only when the owner stops it. Phase5.md:127.
