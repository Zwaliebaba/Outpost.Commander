Structures

Common rules:

  • The base numbers come from Tuning.json: structures, lines 40–63.
  • Hit points at level L are base × (research factor + 20% × (L − 1)), with levelHitPointsPercent 20 (Tuning.json:11; Simulation.cpp:3153-3157; ADR-064 §Decision 4). The table also gives each level with Reinforced Structures (+25%, Tuning.json:90-91).
  • Levels. Only the Command Station, the Shipyard and the Research Lab have levels, up to MAXIMUM_STRUCTURE_LEVEL 5 (Tuning.h:155-156; Tuning.cpp:315-325). A kind's top level is 1 + the length of its levels list (Tuning.h:185-188).
  • An upgrade is paid when it is ordered and builds itself over the level's buildSeconds. No Constructor and no research shortens it, and the structure keeps working (Simulation.cpp:2253-2282,2650-2668; ADR-064 §Decision 2-3).
  • A level lands whole. The structure keeps its share of its hit points (ADR-064 §Decision 4).
  • A destroyed structure is rebuilt at L1 (ADR-064 §Decision 5).
  • Building. A Constructor builds a structure, which is paid in full when the order is given. The site starts at 1/10 of its hit points and rises with the work (Simulation.cpp:2145-2185,2449-2465; ADR-016 §Decision 4-5).
  • Build time with n Constructors is buildConstructorSeconds ÷ (1 + 0.5 × (n − 1)) ÷ constructor-rate factor (Simulation.cpp:2436-2437).
  • Obstacles. Every structure but the Mining Rig blocks movement (ADR-016 §Decision 8).
  • Sight. An unarmed structure sees 200 m and an armed one 300 m (Simulation.cpp:252-262).
  • The panel's next-level line reads "L{n}: {gives}{hit points} hit points" (Hud.cpp:2645-2699).

Command Station (CommandStation)

The Command Station is not buildable: it has no cost or buildConstructorSeconds (Tuning.json:40; Tuning.h:167-169; Simulation.cpp:2150-2151). Each player's is placed built and armed on its start (Simulation.cpp:729-747; ADR-016 §Decision 3). Its footprint is 45 m and its armor 10 at every level, and it carries the Defence gun (structureWeapon 1).

It also does three things at every level:

  • Builds Constructors: 60 Ore and 15 s each, up to 5 queued (Simulation.cpp:2231-2247; Snapshot.h:42).

Retreat (all ships)

  • The trigger. A hit that leaves a ship below its threshold, Half (50%) or Quarter (25%), sends it to its repairer. The repairer is the nearest Repair Bay, else the Command Station, else the nearest Shipyard. With none, the ship fights on (Simulation.cpp:2554-2589; ADR-075 §Decision 5).
  • Where it stops. 40 m beyond the repairer's footprint (Simulation.cpp:86).
  • The end. The retreat ends when the ship is whole, or when its player gives it an order (ADR-075 §Decision 6).
