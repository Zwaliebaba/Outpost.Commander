AI behavior

The AI is AiPlayer (Opponent/AiPlayer.cpp).

  • In a skirmish it plays player 2 as a client on the game's main thread (WinMain.cpp:425-437).
  • In a world it plays as an AiEmpire, on the server's thread (ADR-079 §Decision 3).
  • Cadence. It watches every snapshot for shots, and decides once a second (AiPlayer.cpp:322-333, ADR-020 §Decision 1).
  • No cheating. It reads only its own snapshot (ADR-002 §Decision 7).
