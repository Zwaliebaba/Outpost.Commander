Away

Purpose. What happened while the deputy played the player's seat (Phase 5 design §11). Only a world's seats have deputies (OutpostServer/Main.cpp:193-199; InProcessServer.cpp:512-533), so the window comes only in a world; a skirmish's seat has none. - Opens itself with the first snapshot that carries the report (GameClient.cpp:539-544). - Once closed, the report is gone (GameClient.cpp:763-765).

Geometry (Hud.cpp:1983-2007). - Size. 600 × (36 + 12 + 30 + 22·n + 12), with n ≤ 20 lines (AWAY_LINES = 4 + 16, Hud.h:539). - Place. Centred on the screen. Example: n = 6 gives 660, 429, 600, 222.
