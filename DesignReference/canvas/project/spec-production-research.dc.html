Research

Purpose. The player's Research Lab, its queue, and each topic not yet researched or queued, tier by tier. A topic that needs something is hatched and says what it needs (Phase 1 design §12, Phase 3 design §6, ADR-061 d4, ADR-068 d1).

Geometry (Hud.cpp:1786-1884). - The window is 560 wide. Topic cards are 252 wide, two to a line, at x 24 or 284, y 104 + line·(TH + 8). It shows 10 topics, scrolling by a row (TOPIC_COLUMNS, TOPICS_SHOWN, Hud.h:400-401; StepTopics, Hud.cpp:2991-2999). - Card height. TH = 28 + lines·18 + 4, where lines is the most any topic needs: its effect's wrapped lines plus max(1, its needs). It is at least 2. - With Tuning.json, TH = 86. "Constructor build and repair rate +25%" wraps to 2 lines in the Detail face, and with its need, "RESEARCH LAB L3", that makes 3. - Height. CardsBottom + 20 + 20 + jobs·34 + 20. For page 1 with two jobs: 510, 178, 560, 694.
