The match HUD

Purpose. The HUD shows the player's state at a glance: Ore, what production and research are doing, the territory, alerts, where the match runs, the selection with its commands, and the minimap (ADR-015 d4, ADR-043, ADR-066, ADR-086, ADR-088). It is laid out afresh every frame by Hud::Lay (Hud.cpp:3075-3689) from Hud::Describe's content (Hud.cpp:2453-2834) and from what GameClient::Update adds (GameClient.cpp:719-772).

Layers and clicks. - Entity bars. The bars over entities are drawn first, under every panel (layer −1, Layout::bars). They take no click and cover nothing (Hud.cpp:3095-3126; Hud.h:730-732). - The HUD is layer 0. Each window is a further layer, back to front (Hud.h:714-760). - Draw order. Within a layer: panels, then lines, then sprites, then texts (GameClient.cpp:1300-1336). - A press on any HUD panel is the HUD's. It never reaches the world, while releases always do (GameClient.cpp:681-688; Layout::Covers Hud.cpp:2363-2366; ADR-015 d5). - Clicks go to the front layer only. A HUD button under a window takes no click (ActionAt Hud.cpp:2368-2377). - Hover. The action under the pointer is found in the last frame's layout (GameClient.cpp:711-712). It lights the HUD's enabled button, the retreat cell, the ship bar, the status line and the banner button that take it (ADR-088 d1; Hud.cpp:3129-3131).

Anchors. These are at factor 1.0; at another factor, multiply every value by it (§ Interface scale).

The column

The Ore, status, territory and alerts panels share one width, columnUnits (Hud.cpp:3134-3139; ADR-066 d3). It is the largest of three values: - 380 (COLUMN_LEAST_UNITS, Hud.cpp:132); - the longest research topic's name (Snapshot::research[].nameUtf8) in the Name face, plus the width of +99 in the Figure face, plus 6, plus 24; - the widest of three fixed lines in the Name face, plus 8 and 24 (Hud.cpp:133-135): Shipyards · BUILDING 99 · WAITING 99 · IDLE 99, Shipyards at the fleet cap · Station upgrading, 100% and Research: every open topic done.

≈380 by estimate: the widest line comes to ≈364, under the floor. The real figure depends on Bahnschrift's metrics and is not stated anywhere; ADR-066 gives only the 380 floor.

Alerts panel

Purpose. What the player should know about fronts it is not looking at (ADR-059 d1-d2). The newest alert is a warning chip (ADR-085 d1).

What shows. - The alerts of the last 8 seconds (Alerts::SHOWN_SECONDS, Alerts.h:15), at most 4 (SHOWN_ALERTS, Alerts.h:18), newest first. - One kind of alert in one sector is not raised again within 20 s (REPEAT_SECONDS, Alerts.h:16), except a fired order (Alerts.cpp:57-60).

Geometry. - Panel: 16, 170 + territory height + 8, column, 20 + 22·n. Without a territory panel it starts at y 170 (Hud.cpp:3219-3224). - Newest line: a chip whose tag starts at x 8, so its words line up with the others at x 12 (Hud.cpp:3228-3233). - Older lines: in the Name face, TEXT_COLOR, at x 12. - Lines take no click: the panel adds no press (Hud.cpp:3217-3238), but like any panel it covers clicks. - Space moves the camera to the newest alert (GameClient.cpp:661-666; KeyBindings.h:36). - On the minimap, each alert is a 14-unit square outlined in WARNING_COLOR (Hud.cpp:3608-3617).

Texts (Alerts.cpp:76-98; {sector} is SectorView::nameUtf8, or the field, Alerts.cpp:73): - Relay suppressed: {sector} - Relay under attack: {sector} - Mining Rig lost: {sector} - Enemy ships in {sector} or Pirate ships in {sector} - Ship retreating: {sector} - Pirates cleared: {sector} - Order fired: {action}, Order held back: {action} or Order refused: {action} (Alerts.cpp:32-45), where {action} is one of move to {}, attack-move to {}, attack in {}, hold {}, patrol to {} or Mining Rig in {} (Alerts.cpp:12-29).

Example (hud-busy-warships.json): panel 16, 264, ≈380, 86; chip Enemy ships in B2, with its tag at 24, 274, ≈134, 22.
