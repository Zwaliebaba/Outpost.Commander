8.1 The three files, field by field

There are three files: OpponentEasy.json (Easy), Opponent.json (Normal) and OpponentHard.json (Hard). All three use the same line numbers for every field (verified with grep -n). A field marked ◆ differs from Normal in that file. The Notes column gives the validation in AiSettings.cpp.

  • 12 fields differ in at least one file: attackGroupShips, retreatLossShare, reviewIntervalSeconds, constructors, incomePerShipyardOrePerSecond, contestedAsteroids, shipyardQueueJobs, raidShips, attackWithoutLeadShare, claimSectors, attackCapShare, pirateMargin.

  • The other 19 fields, including researchOrder and all 24 counters, are identical in all three (diff -u).

What each difficulty means (ADR-065):

  • Easy: "the same rules with a smaller hand" (ADR-065 §Decision 2).
  • Hard: "fights harder for territory" (ADR-065 §Decision 3).
  • Neither cheats: "An AI that is helped by the rules … is a new decision" (ADR-065 §What this forecloses).
  • Where each is picked:
  • On the menu: "Easy skirmish", "Normal skirmish", "Hard skirmish" (Hud.cpp:2329-2331).
  • For a world's AI empire: --ai <player>:Easy|Normal|Hard (OutpostServer/Main.cpp:120-133).
  • The default is Normal, both on the client (GameClient.h:271) and for --ai without a difficulty (Main.cpp:125).
  • When they load. All three files are read with the first match's server, before the window shows (WinMain.cpp:281-291, ADR-065 §Decision 4).
