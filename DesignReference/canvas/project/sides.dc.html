Side colors are linear RGB in Models.json, read by ModelCatalog (ModelCatalog.h:20-25: "The color is linear and provisional until team colors are decided (design §15)"). hex = sRGB encoding of the value. The derived colors are computed by A5 from the formulas in GameApp/SceneShading.h/.cpp and GameClient.cpp, unlit (before the scene light, fog and bloom). The player-to-set map is absolute by player number: Models.json:53-56 (player 1 Human, player 2 Tarkan), "pirates": "Pirate" (:57), "derelicts": "Wreck" (:58).

The other derived colors:

  • Rocks: the face is ×0.15 #413E3C, and the line is EdgeColor(1.35, 0) #B7B0AB (SceneShading.h:46,50). A field's rocks take ×0.7 first (SceneShading.h:51, GameClient.cpp:1522).
  • Tints: a Relay is ×1.6 on the ResearchLab model, and a RepairBay ×0.6 on the Shipyard model (Models.json:70-71). A structure being built is darker, by ×(0.35 + 0.65 × the share built) (GameClient.cpp:101,342-349).

  • Ion #95EDFF
  • Fusion #FFA0ED
  • Pulse #C4FF95
  • the Constructor #E7E7E1

The HUD does not read Models.json.

  • Its minimap and territory figures use OWN_, ENEMY_, PIRATE_ and DERELICT_COLOR, which are relative to the viewer.
  • The scene's sets are absolute by player number (ModelCatalog.cpp:304-316).
  • The two families differ in value for every side. Own #A0D3FF is not the same blue as Human #76ADF9. See Open.

Checks against the ADRs: the Human ship line (0.473, 0.637, 1.000) and the Tarkan ship line (1.000, 0.531, 0.437) match ADR-040 decision 1's (0.47, 0.64, 1.0) and (1.0, 0.53, 0.44). Tinted structures: Relay = the Research Lab's L1 mesh at tint 1.6 (Models.json:70), Repair Bay = the Shipyard's L1 mesh at tint 0.6 (Models.json:71); the tint multiplies the set's color before TowardGray (GameClient.cpp:344-349, 1577), so a Human Relay's color is (0.288, 0.672, 1.000) before graying, its blue clamped. Each has no levels in Tuning.json (structures[5], structures[6]), so it draws its model's L1 (DrawnLevel, GameApp/ModelCatalog.h:158-161).

Which models each set renders, and why:

  • Human, Tarkan: all 9 models of the set, the three levelled ones as 5 levels each (21 meshes), plus Relay and Repair Bay (tinted, above): 23 entries each.
  • Pirate: Tarkan's 21 meshes in the Pirate violet, since "meshes": "Tarkan" gives the set all of Tarkan's models (ModelCatalog.cpp:223-230) and LoadClientAssets loads every one. In play the simulation spawns pirates only as Defense Platforms (GameLogic/Simulation.cpp:843) and ships (Simulation.cpp:868) of hull 1 and hull 2 (Tuning.json pirates.outposts[].ships); no other spawn site in GameLogic/ gives PIRATES anything, so the pirates' Command Station, Shipyard, Research Lab, Mining Rig, Large hull and Constructor are loaded but never drawn in a match. No Relay or Repair Bay for Pirate (only players build them).
  • Wreck: only the three hulls, Small, Medium, Large, from the Human meshes in gray: a derelict is drawn by its hull's model (GameClient.cpp:1628-1639, ADR-074, ADR-011 decision 3), although "meshes": "Human" gives the Wreck set all 21 Human meshes (and loads them). A derelict is drawn with ship colors (FILL_SHADE), scaled to 2 × radius / lengthMeters.
  • Asteroids: Small, Medium, Large, in the ore-asteroid look.
