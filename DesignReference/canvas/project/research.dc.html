Research tree, tier by tier

Rules.

  • Tier 1 is open from the start. A Research Lab at L2 opens tier 2, and one at L3 opens tier 3 (ADR-033 §Decision 2; Tuning.cpp:703-712).
  • A topic is refused while its tier is not open (LevelTooLow, Simulation.cpp:3190-3192), or while a prerequisite is neither researched nor queued ahead of it (Simulation.cpp:3181-3189).
  • A topic is paid when it starts (Simulation.cpp:3062-3067).
  • An upgrade applies at once to every existing ship and structure of the player (Simulation.cpp:3105-3141).
  • Percents on one rate add (ADR-033 §Decision 3).
  • The research window lists topics tier by tier. A topic not yet open is dim, and its first need reads "RESEARCH LAB L2" or "RESEARCH LAB L3" (Hud.cpp:2969-2986). Each card reads "TIER n · s" (Hud.cpp:2978).
  • The effect texts below are EffectText's (Research.cpp:142-179).

Topic IDs 9 and 18, the gateways Relay Archives and Precursor Vault, are gone and their IDs are not reused (ADR-033 §Decision 2). "req" names a topic's prerequisites by ID; each entry gives its Ore and its research time. Every line's source is Tuning.json: research[i], lines 66–111.

What each kind of upgrade applies to (Research.h:5-28; ADR-033 §Decision 4):

  • Hull hit points go on all hulls, before the drive's factor. They do not reach the Constructor or structures.
  • Fire rate divides that weapon's interval, from its next shot; a reload under way is not shortened.
  • Structure hit points go on every structure, whose standing ones keep their share.
  • The Defence gun's fire rate divides its interval when it fires.
  • Ship speed goes on every warship, through its hull speed, and on the Constructor. Turn rates are unchanged, and a move under way keeps its pace.
  • The Constructor's rate raises both its build work and its repair.
  • Shipyard build speed speeds Shipyard jobs only, not the Command Station's.
  • Mining income and ore reserve are economy; see A1.
