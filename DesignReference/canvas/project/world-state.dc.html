13.1 The world log (World.log)

One line per record; every time is in ticks (WorldLog.h:29-51).

Record Format
start (new) start <tick> new
start (recovered) start <tick> recovered save <tick> replayed <n>
save save <tick> bytes <n> encode_us <us>
seat seat <tick> player <p> player\|deputy\|ai (on a change, and from the first tick)
order order <tick> player <p> order <id> move\|attack-move\|attack\|hold\|patrol\|rig given\|held\|refused
lost lost <tick> player <p>
waiting waiting <tick> player <p>
restart restart <tick> player <p>
hour hour <tick> player <p> ore <n> income <hundredths/s> fleet <cp> cap <n> nodes <n> researched <n> present <s> deputy <s>, every 3,600 s
battle battle <start> <end> sector <id> player <p> <play> player <p> <play>
  • A battle is two seats firing at each other in one sector within 10 s of each other. It ends after 30 s without such a shot. A side is "present" when its player played at any of its shots. Shots at or by pirates are not a battle (WorldLog.h:55-57, ADR-082 §Decision 2).
  • The log is not saved. A battle the server did not see begin is not recorded (ADR-082 §Consequences).
