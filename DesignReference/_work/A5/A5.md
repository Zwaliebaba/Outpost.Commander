## How a color reaches the screen (applies to every hex below)

- **Every color constant is linear.** The back buffer is `R8G8B8A8_UNORM` (`Renderer.cpp:22`). It is written through an `R8G8B8A8_UNORM_SRGB` render-target view (`Renderer.h:25`, `Renderer.cpp:658-661`), so the hardware encodes the shader's linear output to sRGB. `UiPS.hlsl:4` says so: "The color is linear; the render target encodes it to sRGB."
- **So the hex to draw with is the sRGB encoding of the constant.** The `hex` column below is exactly that.
- **Blending is straight alpha** (`UiPipeline.cpp:110-116`). Through an sRGB view it happens in linear light. A translucent constant (alpha < 1) is shown composited where its ground is known (`hexComposited` in tokens.json).
- **HUD and window colors are final.** They are drawn after the scene's glow is added (§Rules), so nothing changes them.
- **Scene colors (`GameClient.cpp`, Models.json) are not final.** They are multiplied by the scene light:
  - a line takes the luminance of the light (`MeshLinePS.hlsl:42`);
  - a face takes the light per channel (`MeshPS.hlsl:43`);
  - then the bloom is added in the resolve (ADR-091).
- **A scene hex is the unlit constant.** For a mark lying flat on the ground (normal up), tokens.json also gives `hexLitFlat`. A flat line is multiplied by 0.8846 and a flat face by (0.8951, 0.8767, 0.9320), computed by A5 from `SceneShading.cpp:7-25`. ADR-090 decision 3 gives the face light as (0.895, 0.877, 0.932).

How the faces are made:

- **None installed:** a face none of whose families is installed throws, and the game stops (`GlyphAtlas.cpp:229-246`; ADR-030 d2).
- **Rasterizing:** DirectWrite draws grayscale coverage, `NATURAL_SYMMETRIC` (`GlyphAtlas.cpp:288-290`), at `max(1, round(units × scale))` px (`UiPipeline.cpp:28-31`). The atlas is drawn again when the scale moves any face by a whole pixel (ADR-015 d3).
- **Characters:** printable ASCII plus U+00B7 `·` and U+00D7 `×`. Anything else shows `?` (`GlyphAtlas.h:62-67`).
- **Whole pixels:** advances, the pen, the top and the tracking are whole pixels (`GlyphAtlas.cpp:276`; `UiPipeline.cpp:270-272`).
- **Line box:** the line box is `ceil(ascent + descent + lineGap)` from the font's metrics (`GlyphAtlas.cpp:258-259`). Its value per face is **Not specified**: it depends on the installed font.

## Spacing and frame

**The reference frame** is 1920×1080 units (Hud.h:23-24; ADR-006 d5).

- **The scale** is `factor × min(w/1920, h/1080)` (Hud.cpp:3029-3033).
- **On a non-16:9 screen** the spare room goes between the anchors, because each anchor is placed in `width/scale` × `height/scale` units (Hud.cpp:3085-3086).
- **The interface factor** (ADR-070) steps through **1.0, 1.1, 1.25, 1.5, 1.75 and 2.0** (Hud.h:861). It starts at 1.0, and Ctrl+= and Ctrl+- step it.
  - It steps up only while the designer (laid out for every component), the Controls window, and the orders and away windows at their tallest all fit between the margins. On the menu, the Controls window alone must fit (Hud.h:863-866; Hud.cpp:3035-3054).
  - It comes down to the largest step that fits when the screen size changes or a match starts or ends (Hud.cpp:3056-3073).
- **What the factor does not scale:** the entity bars (`Scale` is called without the factor, Hud.cpp:3096), the selection ring (GameClient.cpp:1443) and the stars (ADR-070 d7).
- **Pixel snapping:**
  - Snapped to whole pixels: text, sprites (`UiPipeline.cpp:270-272, 304`) and the entity bars (Hud.cpp:3098-3106).
  - Not snapped: panels and 1-unit outlines (`UiPipeline.cpp:236-241`). See Open.

**What ADR-085 actually enforces** is three things:

- a warning is a chip, which no other element draws;
- build progress is gray;
- Ore's glyph is a gem, which no other element shares.

ADR-085 "What this forecloses": "A warning written as plain text in `WARNING_COLOR` beside an enemy figure. A chip in any other color, or behind anything but a warning."

Two plain-text warnings remain by decision: a refused name (Hud.cpp:1392) and the menu notice (Hud.cpp:2351), both in ADR-085 d1. See Open for the hue families that more than one meaning shares.

### The interface never glows (ADR-091 d1, "forecloses")

The code guarantees it by the frame's order:

1. **The scene, then the interface.** `WinMain.cpp:514-518` calls `BeginFrame`, then `client.Render` (the scene), then `renderer.BeginInterface()`, then `client.RenderInterface`, then `EndFrame`.
2. **The glow is made before any interface exists.** `Renderer::BeginInterface` (`Renderer.cpp:416-450`) makes the bloom from the scene target's shader view alone (`m_bloom->Draw(..., ShaderViewGpu(m_sceneView), ...)`, `Renderer.cpp:435`). It then resolves the scene with the glow added into the back buffer.
3. **The UI draws onto the finished image.** `UiPipeline`'s pipeline state renders straight into the back buffer at one sample with no depth (`UiPipeline.cpp:131-138`). Nothing reads the back buffer back into the bloom.
4. **There is no other path.** `EndFrame` calls `BeginInterface` if nothing did (`Renderer.cpp:464`), so the order holds on every frame.

The entity bars are part of the UI layout, so they never glow either (GameClient.cpp:1297-1299). The selection ring, the footprint rings, the beams and the exhausts are scene, and they do glow (ADR-091 consequences).

### Ore is written one way (ADR-043 d3)

- **The form:** the gem then the figure, grouped in thousands, in `GOLD_COLOR`, through `Painter::GemAndFigure` and `GemAndFigureFrom` (Hud.cpp:1216-1235).
- **Where:** the stockpile (Hud.cpp:3145, the word "Ore" dropped), a window's Ore box (:1248), a HUD button's cost (:1322), a part card's cost (:1465), the Queue cost (:1597), a production option's cost (:1751) and a topic's cost (:1864).
- **Fixed gem place:** the stockpile is written from the panel's left, so the gem stays put as the figure grows (ADR-046; Hud.cpp:3145).
- **Exceptions:** see Open.

## Open / unclear

- **Body face.** Segoe UI 600 at 20 is rasterized (Hud.cpp:3010), and `Hud::Text` defaults to it (Hud.h:670), but no text draws in it at HEAD. **Not specified** whether it is kept on purpose.
- **Pressed state.** No button, card or cell changes on mouse-down. **Not specified.**
- **Front and back windows.** They look the same. **Not specified.**
- **The HUD's side colors are not data.**
  - `OWN_`, `ENEMY_` and `PIRATE_COLOR` are Hud.cpp constants relative to the viewer. The scene's colors come from Models.json, absolute by player number (`ModelCatalog.cpp:304-316`).
  - Rule 6 of the brief says "colors come from data". The minimap and territory figures do not.
  - For a seat whose set is Tarkan, the minimap would show its own forces blue while the world draws them red-orange. Whether a human player can hold seat 2 at HEAD was not checked by A5 (A3's domain). **Not specified.**
- **Hue families shared by more than one meaning.** No document reconciles these beyond ADR-085's three decisions and interface plan 1's note that the warm colors are "each used consistently" (`ImplementationPlan-Interface.md:285`).
  - **Green:** selection ring, healthy, better or Good, commit (Queue, SAVED), valid ghost.
  - **Warm:** Ore gold, waiting amber, Fair, hurt, armed-order ring.
  - **Red:** enemy, worse or Poor, low health, invalid ghost.
  - **Blue:** own, accent, own progress, window edge, pick.
- **Ore written as a word.**
  - The designer's Cost bar writes "87" with the unit "ore" in lowercase: `std::to_string`, not grouped in thousands (Hud.cpp:778).
  - The damage card writes "34.5 / 100 ore" (Hud.cpp:802).
  - Hints write "Derelict: 1,200 Ore" (Hud.cpp:2137) and "Ore left 1,234" (Hud.cpp:2594).
  - ADR-043 d3's list does not name the bars or the hints. Whether these are meant exceptions is **Not specified.**
- **Line boxes.** The line box of each face in pixels depends on the installed font's metrics, so it is **Not specified** in code or docs.
- **Unsnapped outlines.** Panels and 1-unit outlines are not snapped to pixels (`UiPipeline.cpp:236-241`). At 1280×720 a 1-unit outline is 0.667 px. How it rasterizes is **Not specified** by any ADR.
- **The gem is scaled.** It is rasterized at 12 units (Hud.cpp:3022) and drawn at 13 or 8 units, so the atlas bitmap is scaled by bilinear sampling (UiPipeline.cpp:290-305). This is recorded, not judged.
- **Undocumented meanings.** `NEUTRAL_COLOR`, `SLOT_FILLED_COLOR` and `AMBER_COLOR` have no comment in Hud.cpp. Their meanings above are read from their use sites and, for amber, from the Phase 1 design.
- **Dead `CARD_COLOR` arguments.** `CARD_COLOR` is passed as the area color to `AddButton` (Hud.cpp:1979, 2357, 3348, 3439), which ignores it and picks its own face.
- **The title parameter.** `OpenWindow`'s Title-face title is never drawn: every caller passes "".
- **The interface steps.** The brief names 100/110/125%. The code and ADR-070 d2 have six steps, to 200%.
  - ADR-070's consequences compute the reachable steps from the designer alone: 110% in a match on 16:9, 125% on the menu.
  - `WindowsFit` now also counts the orders and away windows (Hud.cpp:3048). A5 did not recompute the reachable steps.
- **The mockup's title-bar hatch, its gradients and its "SAVED" green** differ from the code with no recorded decision (Mockup vs code, rows 5, 10, 11 and 14).
