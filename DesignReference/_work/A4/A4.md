**Conventions.**
- **Rect.** A rect is `x, y, w, h` in pixels of a 1920×1080 back buffer, origin top-left. At interface factor 1.0 those are the HUD's reference units (Hud.h:23-24, `Hud::Scale` Hud.cpp:3029-3033). Inside a window, positions are given in **window units from the window's top-left corner**, as `Painter` places them (Hud.cpp:1096-1135), with the window's default corner stated once.
- **Colors** are the constant names in `GameApp/Hud.cpp`, and agent A5 resolves their values. `OWN_COLOR@0.12` means that constant at that alpha.
- **Faces** are `Hud::Typeface` (Hud.h:32-41), sized by `FACE_UNITS` (Hud.h:46), from `Hud::Typefaces` (Hud.cpp:3003-3018):

  | Face | Size | Font |
  |---|---|---|
  | Title | 22 | Bahnschrift 700, SemiCondensed |
  | Label | 13 | Bahnschrift 600 |
  | Name | 16 | Bahnschrift 600 |
  | Figure | 14 | Cascadia Mono 600, or Consolas where it is not installed |
  | LargeFigure | 28 | Cascadia Mono 700, or Consolas |
  | Detail | 13 | Cascadia Mono 400, or Consolas |
  | Body | 20 | Segoe UI 600 |

  Body is rasterized, but no drawn text uses it: `grep Typeface::Body` finds only Hud.cpp:3010. "Tracking 2" marks spaced capitals, 2 units between letters (Hud.cpp:954, `Painter::Tracking` Hud.cpp:1109-1112).
- **Labels.** Labels are verbatim from the code. `{…}` is a field the code formats, and its format string is given. Example values in the JSON carry `"sample": true`, and are built from `OutpostCommander/Assets/*.json` where they can be.
- **Every panel of the HUD is in the windows' look** (ADR-043 decision 1): a `WINDOW_COLOR` body and four `Corner` sprites, 12 units, in `WINDOW_EDGE_COLOR`, mirrored to each corner (`Frame` Hud.cpp:903-908, `AddCorners` Hud.cpp:852-866). The tables below do not repeat the corners.

## 2. Startup (loading) and the moment before a match

- **Not specified: a loading screen.** None exists. The window is created hidden, and shown once four things are ready (WinMain.cpp:273-316; ADR-049 decisions 2-3):
  - the first server and the AI settings, made in parallel;
  - the client's assets;
  - the interface atlas;
  - the GPU uploads.

  ADR-049's Consequences "forecloses a window that shows anything before the game has loaded", and leave a loading screen open.
- **A failure while loading** shows a Windows message box captioned `Outpost Commander`, with the error, and the game exits (WinMain.cpp:22, 95-101, 559-572). This is not a game screen.
- **Switches that skip the menu.**
  - `--measure`, `--load` and `--stress` start a match at once (WinMain.cpp:251-252, 369-370). Only `--measure` alone plays the Normal AI; under `--load` or `--stress` no AI is made (WinMain.cpp:329-334).
  - `--join <file>` joins at once (WinMain.cpp:226-235, 367-368).
- **A match before its first snapshot** (`match-awaiting-first-snapshot.json`, no elements).
  - `StartMatch` clears the layout (GameClient.cpp:472-478, 517).
  - `Update` lays the HUD out only once the view holds a snapshot (GameClient.cpp:689-775), and `DrawHud` draws nothing while `fontPixels` is 0 (GameClient.cpp:1291-1293).
  - So the screen shows the sky and the grid only (GameClient.cpp:1138-1158).
  - The camera centres on the player's ships with the first snapshot (GameClient.cpp:548-567).
- **Joining a world is synchronous.**
  - `JoinWorld` builds the `QuicTransport` inside the frame loop (WinMain.cpp:505-508 → 349-358).
  - Its constructor waits up to `WELCOME_TIMEOUT`, 10,000 ms, for the server's welcome (QuicTransport.cpp:9, 26-35).
  - No frame is drawn meanwhile, so the last menu frame stays on screen. **Not specified: a "connecting" indicator.**

## 3. The match HUD

**Purpose.** The HUD shows the player's state at a glance: Ore, what production and research are doing, the territory, alerts, where the match runs, the selection with its commands, and the minimap (ADR-015 d4, ADR-043, ADR-066, ADR-086, ADR-088). It is laid out afresh every frame by `Hud::Lay` (Hud.cpp:3075-3689) from `Hud::Describe`'s content (Hud.cpp:2453-2834) and from what `GameClient::Update` adds (GameClient.cpp:719-772).

**Layers and clicks.**
- **Entity bars.** The bars over entities are drawn first, under every panel (layer −1, `Layout::bars`). They take no click and cover nothing (Hud.cpp:3095-3126; Hud.h:730-732).
- **The HUD is layer 0.** Each window is a further layer, back to front (Hud.h:714-760).
- **Draw order.** Within a layer: panels, then lines, then sprites, then texts (GameClient.cpp:1300-1336).
- **A press on any HUD panel is the HUD's.** It never reaches the world, while releases always do (GameClient.cpp:681-688; `Layout::Covers` Hud.cpp:2363-2366; ADR-015 d5).
- **Clicks go to the front layer only.** A HUD button under a window takes no click (`ActionAt` Hud.cpp:2368-2377).
- **Hover.** The action under the pointer is found in the last frame's layout (GameClient.cpp:711-712). It lights the HUD's enabled button, the retreat cell, the ship bar, the status line and the banner button that take it (ADR-088 d1; Hud.cpp:3129-3131).

**Anchors.** These are at factor 1.0; at another factor, multiply every value by it (§ Interface scale).

### 3.10 Minimap

**Purpose.** The whole map: whose sectors are whose, the marks, the fog, the alerts, and the camera's view (ADR-015 d4, ADR-043 d7, ADR-068 d2, ADR-081).

**Geometry** (Hud.cpp:3456-3465).
- The frame is `16, 764, 300, 300`; the map area, `MAP_COLOR`, is `24, 772, 284, 284`.
- **Scale.** 284 units for `Snapshot::mapSizeMeters`, 10,000 m with Map.json `sizeMeters`: 0.0284 units a metre, or 35.2 m a unit.
- **Orientation.** +x runs right and +z up (Hud.cpp:2432-2451).

**Draw order and looks** (all in layer 0's panels; the view's segments are lines).
1. **Sector wash.** Every held or pirate-guarded sector, filled in `OWN_COLOR`, `ENEMY_COLOR` or `PIRATE_COLOR` at alpha 0.12 (Hud.cpp:3481-3485, 42). A sector is 56.8 units square on Map.json's 5×5 grid of 2,000 m sectors.
2. **Marks.** The neutral ones are drawn first, then the sided ones (Hud.cpp:3486-3528). A mark is a square of side max(least, 2·radius·0.0284). The least sides are: a ship 3, an ore asteroid 8, anything else 6 (Hud.cpp:118-120). The map's asteroids and fields are always seen (Simulation.cpp:245-247), so every frame in a match has all 80 fields and all 40 ore asteroids: Map.json's 6 home ones and the 34 the seed places (Map.cpp:381-419), under the fog. An asteroid is dry only once the player knows its reserve is 0, which it learns in sight or in a sector it holds (Simulation.cpp:331-342). A derelict is seen as an enemy structure is, in sight or in a held, unsuppressed sector (Simulation.cpp:289-304, 2981-2987), and remembered after.

**Slots.** A production or research window not yet moved takes the first of the two slots that no open window holds; moving it frees its slot (WindowManager.cpp:6-21, 67-75; task 15.4). At factor 1.0, production, research and the designer stand side by side with nothing to spare: 16 + 478 + 16 + 560 + 16 + 818 + 16 = 1920 (ADR-062 d6). `window-three-windows-default.json` lays out all three.

**Layering** (ADR-031 d4).
- Windows are drawn back to front in `WindowManager` order (Hud.cpp:3636-3637). Without a manager, as in the tests, the order is Production, Research, Designer, Controls, Orders, Away (Hud.cpp:3634-3635).
- A window covers the text of the windows behind it.
- A left press anywhere on a window brings it to the front (GameClient.cpp:998; WindowManager.cpp:89-93).
- Only the front layer under the pointer takes a click.

**Drag** (ADR-031 d2, d5).
- A left press on the title bar, where no button sits, grabs the window (GameClient.cpp:993-996). It follows the pointer until the button is released (GameClient.cpp:978-982, 1024-1025; WindowManager.cpp:67-87).
- **Kept on screen** by `Hud::KeepOnScreen` (Hud.cpp:2420-2426): x is held between 120 − w and screenWidth − 120 (`WINDOW_KEPT_ON_SCREEN_UNITS`, Hud.h:646), and y between 0 and screenHeight − 36.
- `GameClient` settles the manager on the clamped place every frame (GameClient.cpp:773-774; WindowManager.cpp:56-60).

**Close.**
- The close box (GameClient.cpp:987-991).
- **Esc** closes the front window and goes no further (GameClient.cpp:659-660; WindowManager.cpp:41-47). While the designer's name takes typing, Esc is the designer's first: it drops what was typed (GameClient.cpp:641, 819-835; Designer.cpp:246-250).
- The window's own key toggles it (GameClient.cpp:645-674), but only once the match has a snapshot (GameClient.cpp:667).

The Away window has no key: it opens itself.

**What a window aims at.**
- Opening the designer or production while one structure is selected aims it at that structure (`ToggleWindow`, GameClient.cpp:801-817).
- The selection panel's buttons aim them too (GameClient.cpp:896-899, 917-923).
- Selecting a Shipyard while the designer is open re-aims it, and selecting a producer while production is open re-aims that (GameClient.cpp:698-706).

**Lifetime** (owner, 2026-10-02; ADR-031 d1).
- Positions last until the game closes, and are never written to disk.
- Leaving a match closes every window but keeps their places (WindowManager.cpp:49-54; GameClient.cpp:508).
- The match runs on while any window is open.

## Interface scale

**The steps.** `Hud::INTERFACE_STEPS` are **100, 110, 125, 150, 175 and 200 %** (Hud.h:861; ADR-070 d2). The brief's "100/110/125%" matches what a 16:9 screen reaches (see below), not the list of steps. Ctrl+= takes the next step and Ctrl+- the one before (KeyBindings.h:41-42; GameClient.cpp:779-799). The factor starts at 100% (GameClient.h:347), lasts until the game closes, and is never written to disk (ADR-070 d5).

**What scales.** `Hud::Scale` = factor × min(width/1920, height/1080) (Hud.cpp:3029-3033). Everything the HUD lays out scales with it, and each anchor is computed in the smaller reference screen (width/scale, height/scale):
- the menu, every HUD panel and every window;
- text, which is rasterized again at the new size (ADR-070 d6, `UiPipeline::UseScale` GameClient.cpp:577-579);
- hit-testing and window positions (`ToUnits`, GameClient.cpp:1034-1038).

**What does not scale.**
- The bars over entities (Hud.cpp:3096; ADR-088 d5).
- The selection ring (ADR-067).
- The stars (GameClient.cpp:1140). ADR-070 d7.

**What limits it** (`Hud::WindowsFit`, Hud.cpp:3035-3054; `StepInterface`, 3056-3073).
- **A step up** is taken only while these fit the screen less 2 × 16 margins:
  - the Controls window (700 × 762), always;
  - in a match, also the Orders window (640 × 560), the Away window at 20 lines (600 × 530), and the designer laid out for the match's components (818 × 913 with Tuning.json).
- **A step down** is always taken.
- **Re-fit.** A new screen size, or a match starting or ending, takes the factor down to the largest step that fits (GameClient.cpp:792-798).

**Computed reach.** These figures come from the arithmetic, not from a screen.

| Screen | Where | Reaches | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 16:9 | Menu | **125%** | Controls: 762 + 32 = 794 fits 1080/1.25 = 864, but not 1080/1.5 = 720 |
| 16:9 | Match | **110%** | The designer: 913 + 32 = 945 fits 981.8, but not 864 |
| 2880×1920 | Match | **125%** | ADR-070 says so too |
| 4:3, e.g. 1600×1200 | Match | ≈**150%** | Not stated in any document |

**Effects of scaling.**
- At 110%, the windows overlap at their default places (ADR-070 Consequences). Research's slot 1 ends at 1070 units, past the designer's default x of 911.
- Layout files: `menu-scale-125.json` and `hud-scale-110.json`. In the latter, the selection panel is ≈`589.3, 813.8, 323.4, 248.6` and the minimap `17.6, 732.4, 330, 330`.

## Open / unclear

1. **Every text width is estimated.** All `"measured": false` elements, about 2,300 of 9,265 across the files, and every **≈** above, depend on DirectWrite metrics. Exact values need the optional `GameAppTests` dump on Windows. This includes:
   - the column width (≈380, §3.1);
   - the selection panel's width between its bounds;
   - the tag's width;
   - every right-aligned text's left edge;
   - the menu notice's line breaks.
2. **The designer's seventh performance row runs into its footer.** This is derived from the code and not seen on screen.
   - `ExtentOf` reserves 6 bar rows (`SECTION_LABEL_HEIGHT + 6 × BAR_ROW_HEIGHT`, Hud.cpp:1087).
   - `DescribeDesigner` makes 7 whenever a module has sight: Sensors is added at Hud.cpp:772-777, and Tuning.json `modules[Sensor Array].sightMeters` is 700.
   - So the Build row's top is S + 176, while the footer starts at S + 188. The `Build` label (S + 178 to ≈S + 194) overlaps the top ≈6 units of the RENAME button, and the Build bar's track (S + 182 to S + 189) touches the footer's `RETREAT` label.
   - ADR-061's overlap test compares texts with texts, and these two texts do not meet, so it would not catch this.
   - In the JSON: `designer.bar.build.*` against `designer.rename` (absolute y 851 against 861).
3. **The Large hull card's numbers line is probably cut short at 1080p with Cascadia Mono.**
   - `1,200 HP · ARM 14 · 25m/s` is 25 characters.
   - At 13 px, Cascadia Mono's advance, 1200/2048 em, rounds to 8 px (GlyphAtlas.cpp:276), so the line is 200 px against 196 units of room (Hud.cpp:1459, 1467). It would read `1,200 HP · ARM 14 · 2...`.
   - Cascadia's advance width is quoted from memory and was not verified here. With Consolas (7 px) the line fits, and at 1280×720 it fits.
   - ADR-062 d4 says the hull's numbers fit. This is a possible conflict, unproven.
4. **The picked weapon's name may be cut.** `Mass Driver` in a 78-unit room in the Name face is borderline by estimate, and the JSON shows it cut. Not verified.
5. **No locked module card can appear.** `ViewOf(ModuleTuning)` always sets `available = true` (GameLogic/ShipDesign.cpp:76-83), so the locked-module path (`RESEARCH`, Hud.cpp:716) cannot show with the current code.
6. **Not specified: a loading screen, or a "connecting" indicator for Join world.** None exists. ADR-049 leaves a loading screen open, and the join blocks the frame loop for up to 10 s (§2).
7. **The minimap view outline is an estimate.** Its corners were replayed from Camera.cpp and Camera.json at the default zoom, unturned, with the focus estimated from the starting Constructors' placement. A7 owns the camera.
8. **Sample positions.**
   - Pirate-guarded sectors, ore asteroids other than the 6 home ones, and derelicts are placed by the seed (ADR-072), so their positions in the JSON are samples. They are one seed's, 342: `derive_layouts.py` replays `PlaceContent` (Map.cpp:354-508) with a port of `Neuron::Random` that reproduces RandomTests' pinned draws, rounds each float step as the code does, and checks the map's rules again on the result. It was not checked against a build.
   - Seed 342 puts the camps on the contested pairs D1/B5 and E2/A4, and the strongholds on E1/A5 and C3: the 7 sectors guarded at the start of every match. Its flank derelict in B1 (300 Ore) names Large Hull.
   - The 80 asteroid fields and the 6 home ore asteroids come from Map.json.
9. **Hover lighting is limited.** It applies only to the HUD's own buttons, retreat cells, ship bars, status lines and the banner's `Back to menu` (ADR-088 d1, Hud.cpp:3129-3131). Nothing in a window lights on hover (ADR-088 Consequences), though the designer previews a hovered part. The Orders window's Give button is drawn with `AddButton` but without a hovered flag, so it never lights (Hud.cpp:1979). The menu has no hover either. Whether this is intended for the Give button is **not specified**.
10. **The Segoe UI "Body" face** is rasterized (Hud.cpp:3010) but used by no text. Its size, `FONT_UNITS` 20, only sets `Layout::fontPixels`, which `DrawHud` turns back into the scale (GameClient.cpp:1295).
11. **The scale reach on 4:3 (≈150%)** is my computation. No document states it.
