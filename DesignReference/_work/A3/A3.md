**How a time of day becomes a tick:**

1. The client turns the hour and minute into the next moment after now on the player's clock and sends it as seconds since 1970 UTC (`OrderForm.cpp:217-218`, `ADR-080 §Decision 6`).
2. The host makes it a tick as the command arrives: the first tick at or after the moment, rounded up. A moment past is the current tick. One more than a week ahead is held to a week (`WEEK_SECONDS = 604800`). The tick a client writes is never trusted (`InProcessServer.cpp:338-351, 468-473`).
3. An order that a pause has pushed past its time fires late, at its tick (`Phase5.md:117`).

**The player's clock** is the system's time zone, with summer time, from the standard library's database, or UTC where the database cannot say (`PlayerClock.cpp:45-55`). A reading the clock skips as summer time starts is the moment it skips at; a reading it shows twice as summer time ends is the first (`PlayerClock.h:27-29`, `PlayerClock.cpp:85-89`).

**An event trigger watches one sector the player holds.** An order whose sector is not the map's, or given on a map without territory, is refused as `NoSector` (`Simulation.cpp:3290-3291`).

## 14. Flows

### 14.1 Menu → match → end (skirmish)

1. Launch. Unless `--join` or a measurement switch is given, the menu shows. The first match's server and the three AI files are already loaded (`WinMain.cpp:281-291, 366-370`).
2. The player presses "Easy skirmish", "Normal skirmish" or "Hard skirmish" (`Hud.cpp:2329-2331`).
3. Both seats are taken over QUIC, the AI is made with that difficulty's file, and the server starts. The HUD tag reads "Local skirmish" (`WinMain.cpp:319-344`).
4. Play. Alerts, orders and standing orders work as in §1 to §4.
5. A player loses its production, or its tickets run out. The banner says "Victory", "Defeat" or "Draw", with "[By domination. ]Match length m:ss" (`Hud.cpp:2185-2219`).
6. The world runs on behind the banner (`ADR-020 §Decision 9`).
7. "Back to menu" ends the server, makes the next one and shows the menu (`WinMain.cpp:491-504`). "Quit" closes the game.

### 14.2 Join → play → lost connection

1. The owner runs `OutpostServer --new-world <folder> --host <name>`, then `OutpostServer <folder>`. The server writes `Join-Player<n>.json` (`OutpostServer/Main.cpp:136-206`).
2. The owner hands the player the file. The player puts it at `Documents\Outpost Commander\Join.json`, or runs `--join <file>` (`ADR-078 §Decision 9`).
3. The menu shows "Join world" (`Hud.cpp:2332-2333`). The player presses it.
4. The client connects (10 s handshake), sends the token, and is seated (10 s for the welcome).
   - On failure the menu shows "Could not join the world: …" (`WinMain.cpp:349-364`, `QuicTransport.cpp:19-44`).
5. The HUD tag reads "Server {host}:{port}" (`Hud.cpp:3323`). If the player was away, the away window opens (§5).
6. Snapshots stop.
   - After 2 s: "No word from the server · n s" (`Hud.cpp:3324-3336`).
   - A snapshot ends the warning.
7. The connection closes, or MsQuic's idle timeout ends it (30 s by default). The client goes back to the menu with "The connection to the server was lost: {reason}" (`WinMain.cpp:408-416, 454-466`).
8. The player joins again with the same file, and the newest connection holds the seat (`InProcessServer.cpp:305-329`).

### 14.3 Away → deputy → return

1. The player's connection goes; the server sees it gone (`QuicChannel::HasGone`) (`InProcessServer.cpp:528`).
2. The player still holds the seat for 60 s of ticks (`SeatController.cpp:39-45`).
3. After 60 s the deputy plays.
   - Its turn begins: it counts the Constructors to keep, and clears last turn's defenders (`Deputy.cpp:143-154`).
   - The host starts the away report at this tick (`AwayReports.cpp:49-50`).
4. While away:
   - The deputy keeps research, production, Constructors, rigs and repairs, and defends held sectors, once a second (§6.2).
   - Scheduled orders fire whoever plays (`ADR-079 §Decision 6`).
   - Events go into the report.
   - A save every 60 s keeps the report (`ADR-077 §Decision 5`).
5. If the player's empire falls, it is told (`EmpireLost`) and restarts an hour later once its start is free; the deputy plays on (§13.3).
6. The player takes the seat again (§14.2 step 8). The deputy hands back at once (`SeatController.cpp:37-38`).
7. The first snapshot carries the report, and the window "WHILE YOU WERE AWAY" opens with "Away for h:mm:ss" and its lines (`GameClient.cpp:537-544`, `Hud.cpp:2293-2320`).
8. The player closes the window, and the report is gone (`GameClient.cpp:763-765`). Whatever the deputy ordered stands until the player changes it (`Phase5.md:95`).

## 15. Open / unclear

1. **Selecting enemy ships.** The Controls window says "Click — Select a ship, or a structure of yours" (`KeyBindings.h:94`). The code picks only the player's own ships (`PlayerControls.cpp:251-253`). Whether "a ship" was meant to include enemy ships is **Not specified**.
2. **Number of scheduled orders per seat: Not specified.** No limit was found in `Simulation.cpp:3274-3313`. The window shows the first 6 and "and n more" (`Hud.h:526`, `Hud.cpp:1964-1965`).
3. **Refusals of player commands are invisible.** "The protocol cannot tell the client yet" (`InProcessServer.cpp:482`). A scheduled order refused when it is given (for example `InvalidCondition`) therefore just does not appear in the list. Any player-facing message for this is **Not specified**.
4. **The scout's own-half time counts the world's ticks, not the AI's.** `ownHalf` compares the snapshot's tick with 600 s of ticks (`AiPlayer.cpp:1340`). An AI empire made afresh late in a world, after a server restart or a seat restart, goes straight to the flanks. Whether that is intended is **Not specified** in ADR-076.
5. **Defenders at hand-back.** Once the player plays, the deputy only watches (`Deputy.cpp:128-138`). Warships it sent to defend are not moved back when the seat is handed back mid-defense; its defender list is cleared when its next turn begins (`Deputy.cpp:150`). Phase 5 §6 says only that "What the deputy ordered stands".
6. **The first join of a new world shows an away report** covering the deputy's play from tick 0, by `SeatController.cpp:35-36` and `AwayReports.cpp:49-50`. No document says whether this is intended: **Not specified**.
7. **Hostile starts (gate H6).** Whether a start with enemy warships on it counts as free, and whether a restarted base is spared for a while, is open (`Plan5.md:247`, `ADR-084 §Consequences`).
8. **How long until a vanished client's deputy takes over.** 60 s after the server sees the connection gone (`Server.h:32`), plus MsQuic's default idle timeout of 30 s, which no code sets (`ADR-079 §Decision 4`). The total is not stated anywhere as one figure.
9. **The away window and the record of hand-overs.** The away window shows the time away in the title face only; no other place in the client shows a log of hand-overs. Hand-overs are in `World.log` (`seat` records) only (`ADR-079 §Consequences`).
10. **Owner runs pending:** W1, W4 and W6; W2 in Release; a 02:00 attack from the orders window (W5); a world with an AI empire left for an hour (37.3); the banner and an AI replanning round its new station in play (39.1) (`Plan5.md:28-40, 156, 196, 206, 250`).
