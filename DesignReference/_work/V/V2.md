## Defects not among the 17

A. **The item 10 fix degrades tables canvas-wide** (new). The details are under item 10. Numbers split over lines, such as TRIS "2/5/6", can be misread.

B. **Every held or guarded sector outline is drawn dashed** (present before, missed by V). The composer writes `style = "dashed" if e.get("pattern") else "solid"` (compose/screens.py:105), and the layouts set `pattern: "solid"`, so every outline in all 27 frames comes out dashed. The game draws an outline solid unless the sector is cut off (Hud.cpp:3569-3577). The frames therefore show every sector as cut off. In busy-warships, scale-110, orders-time-of-day and three-windows, the one sector that really is cut off (A3) looks like all the others.

C. **The minimap leaves out structures and warships that the same frame's panels show** (smaller than before, still present). The game marks every entity in the snapshot (Hud.cpp:2533-2541, 3498-3528). The frames mark only the station, Relays, rigs, Constructors and a few ships:
- In shipyard-selected, the selected Shipyard 01 has no mark.
- In lab-needs-research, the selected Lab has no mark.
- designer-default implies 2 Shipyards, a Lab and 4 warships, and none is marked.
- In busy-warships, 3 Shipyards, the Lab and the 40% site are not marked. The 12 selected warships appear as 2 marks.

The makers' claim that "the panels cannot disagree" does not hold for the minimap. The impact is low, because these marks would sit within a few pixels of the station.

D. **Markdown `\|` escapes leak inside code spans** (present before, missed by V). There are 26 on 6 boards. Examples: world `[:Easy\|Normal\|Hard]`, spec-hud-2 `Shipyard\|300` and `{m:ss}\|{cost}`, world-state `player\|deputy\|ai`. The rest are on spec-orders-away, spec-hud-1 and spec-designer.

E. **Minor: held outlines are composited over the wrong color.** The frames composite them over the map ground, which gives #7CA5C8 for own. In the game they are drawn over the opaque LATTICE_COLOR line on the same pixels, which gives #87ADCF (Hud.cpp:3567-3573).
