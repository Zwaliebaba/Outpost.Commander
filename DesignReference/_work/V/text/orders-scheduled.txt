How a time of day becomes a tick:
- The client turns the hour and minute into the next moment after now on the player's clock and sends it as seconds since 1970 UTC (OrderForm.cpp:217-218, ADR-080 §Decision 6).
- The host makes it a tick as the command arrives: the first tick at or after the moment, rounded up. A moment past is the current tick. One more than a week ahead is held to a week (WEEK_SECONDS = 604800). The tick a client writes is never trusted (InProcessServer.cpp:338-351, 468-473).
- An order that a pause has pushed past its time fires late, at its tick (Phase5.md:117).
The player's clock is the system's time zone, with summer time, from the standard library's database, or UTC where the database cannot say (PlayerClock.cpp:45-55). A reading the clock skips as summer time starts is the moment it skips at; a reading it shows twice as summer time ends is the first (PlayerClock.h:27-29, PlayerClock.cpp:85-89).
An event trigger watches one sector the player holds. An order whose sector is not the map's, or given on a map without territory, is refused as NoSector (Simulation.cpp:3290-3291).

6.2 What the deputy does (once a second)
 | Keeper rule
 | Detail
 | Source
 | Research
 | A Lab with a slot free takes the next topic of the AI's research order, then the first topic of the tuning data's that it can take
 | Deputy.cpp:236-264
 | Production
 | A finished Shipyard with an empty queue gets one job: the design it last queued, learned whether the deputy played or watched. A Shipyard it never saw build gets the design most of the player's warships are of, the oldest of a tie. Nothing if the Shipyard's level cannot build it.
 | Deputy.cpp:191-206, 266-307
 | Waiting jobs
 | The job waits for the Ore and the fleet cap as any job does
 | Deputy.cpp:288-289
 | Command Station
 | Upgraded once the Ore is there, when the cap holds a Shipyard's next ship back
 | Deputy.cpp:229-230, 309-323
 | Constructors
 | Keeps as many as the player had when the deputy took the seat, counting those queued at the Command Station (owner, 2026-10-08)
 | Deputy.cpp:143-149, 325-332, Phase5.md:96
 | Rigs
 | Rebuilds a Mining Rig it saw stand, with the 2 nearest idle Constructors, on an asteroid that still holds ore, in a sector the player holds and the pirates do not guard. It waits 3 s to see the site before ordering again.
 | Deputy.cpp:14-18, 334-373
 | Repairs
 | Each damaged structure, and each site nobody builds, gets the nearest idle Constructor
 | Deputy.cpp:375-398
 | Defence
 | Idle warships attack-move at enemy warships, the pirates' among them, in the held sector that has the most; or at whatever fired on a player structure in a held sector. "Idle" means no order, no standing order, not retreating and no scheduled order.
 | Deputy.cpp:53-59, 400-477
 | End of a defence
 | 10 s (defenseHoldSeconds) after the attack was last seen, or at once when the sector is lost. Each defender then moves back to where it stood (owner, 2026-10-08). A defender that stopped more than 150 m short is sent again.
 | Deputy.cpp:20, 437-466, Opponent.json:13
 | Never
 | Attacks, raids, claims a sector, clears pirates or salvages, except by a scheduled order the player gave
 | ADR-079 §Decision 6, Deputy.h:18-19
 | Leaves alone
 | A ship that retreats, or has an order, a standing order or a scheduled order
 | Deputy.cpp:55-59
 | Each turn starts afresh
 | Its Constructor count, repairs and defenders belong to the turn. What it learned while watching (each Shipyard's last design, where the rigs stood) it keeps, but not across a server restart.
 | Deputy.cpp:143-154, ADR-079 §Decision 6, ADR-079 §Consequences
The deputy plays on when its player's seat restarts after a loss; it lays no plan (ADR-084 §Decision 6).
6.3 How the seat comes back
The player joins with its join file, from the menu's "Join world" or --join (§11). The newest connection holds the seat (InProcessServer.cpp:318-321). The deputy hands the seat back at once. The first snapshot carries the away report, and its window opens (§5).
Away → deputy → return
1The player's connection goes
The player's connection goes; the server sees it gone (QuicChannel::HasGone) (InProcessServer.cpp:528).
2The player still holds the seat
The player still holds the seat for 60 s of ticks (SeatController.cpp:39-45).
3After 60 s the deputy plays.
After 60 s the deputy plays.
– Its turn begins: it counts the Constructors to keep, and clears last turn's defenders (Deputy.cpp:143-154).
– The host starts the away report at this tick (AwayReports.cpp:49-50).
4While away:
While away:
– The deputy keeps research, production, Constructors, rigs and repairs, and defends held sectors, once a second (§6.2).
– Scheduled orders fire whoever plays (ADR-079 §Decision 6).
– Events go into the report.
– A save every 60 s keeps the report (ADR-077 §Decision 5).
5If the player's empire falls
If the player's empire falls, it is told (EmpireLost) and restarts an hour later once its start is free; the deputy plays on (§13.3).
6The player takes the seat again
The player takes the seat again (§14.2 step 8). The deputy hands back at once (SeatController.cpp:37-38).
7The first snapshot carries the report
The first snapshot carries the report, and the window "WHILE YOU WERE AWAY" opens with "Away for h:mm:ss" and its lines (GameClient.cpp:537-544, Hud.cpp:2293-2320).
8The player closes the window
The player closes the window, and the report is gone (GameClient.cpp:763-765). Whatever the deputy ordered stands until the player changes it (Phase5.md:95).
