1.3 What each command does
Every command goes to the server, which validates it and applies it at the start of its next tick, or rejects it (Command.h:5-6, ADR-002 §Decision 2). The player is not told of a refusal: "A rejected command changes nothing. The protocol cannot tell the client yet" (InProcessServer.cpp:482).

 | What the player does
 | What the server does

 | Set retreat

 | Command.h:134-140, RetreatThreshold.h:15, ADR-088 §Decision 3
 | Schedule order
 | Orders window, "Give to N ships"
 | §4
 | Command.h:191-202
How orders interact:
- Any order ends a retreat. Any order the player gives a ship, other than setting its retreat, ends the ship's retreat and takes it out of the scheduled order it waits on (Simulation.cpp:3240-3258).
- Move, attack-move, attack and stop end a standing order (Simulation.cpp:3259-3268, ADR-059 §Decision 5).
- A scheduled order does neither when it is given. It does not end a standing order, and it gives its ships nothing to do until it fires (Simulation.cpp:3224-3226).
