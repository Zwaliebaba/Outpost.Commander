Ore gem (ADR-085 d3; ADR-043 d3)
- Shape: a cut gem with the corners (0.28, 0.14), (0.72, 0.14), (0.97, 0.40), (0.50, 0.94) and (0.03, 0.40) of its square, y down. Edges are smoothed over a pixel (GlyphAtlas.cpp:27, 53-56).
- Raster size: 12 units in the atlas (Hud.cpp:3022).
- Drawn size: round(face × 0.6) square, which is 13 units beside the Title face and 8 beside the Figure face.
- It stands 5 units before the figure (ORE_MARK_GAP_UNITS).
- It is centered vertically on face × 1.25.
- Its place comes from GemAndFigure (Hud.cpp:1216-1235).
- Color: GOLD_COLOR. A disabled card's or button's gem takes the dim color of its figure: LOCKED_TEXT_COLOR or DIM_TEXT_COLOR.
- The figure is grouped in thousands (WithThousands, Hud.cpp:2114).
Checkbox (Hud.cpp:3023; GlyphAtlas.cpp:329-334)
- The outline of a square, 10 units, with a stroke of 1/12 of its size (at least 1 px).
- It is drawn in AMBER_COLOR before a lock line or a need line.

Minimap (Hud.cpp:3455-3630)
Frame: a HUD panel 300 square. The map square inside it is 284, 8 in, in MAP_COLOR.
Draw order:
- The sector washes.
- The marks: neutral first, then sided.
- The fog.
- Every sector border, in LATTICE_COLOR.
- The outlines of held and guarded sectors: dashed while cut off, hatched at α 0.35 while suppressed.
- The claimable outlines, 3 in, in OWN_COLOR.
- The memory crosses.
- The alert squares, in WARNING_COLOR.
- The view, in VIEW_COLOR, 1.5 wide.
Marks (:3498-3527):
- A ship is a square of at least 3 units, a structure at least 6, and an ore asteroid at least 8.
- An ore asteroid, dry or not, is a square outlined 2 units wide. Every other mark is filled.
