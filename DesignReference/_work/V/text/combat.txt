Combat rules
 | Rule
 | Detail
 | Source
 | Damage per hit, after armor
 | max(damage × 0.25, damage − armor), in hundredths: max(d/4, d − a), the quarter rounded down to a hundredth
 | DesignStats.h:16-21; MVP §7 "Damage model"; ADR-014 §Context; secondary: Tools/BattleModel.py:348-349
 | Weapons are turrets, and hits are instant
 | Ships fire in any direction and keep firing on the move. Nothing dodges a shot, and its look is presentation.
 | MVP §7 "Combat rules" l.202,212; ADR-014 §What this forecloses
 | Targeting
 | An attack order's target while it is in range. Otherwise the nearest enemy ship in range, or with none in range the nearest enemy structure, kept until it dies or leaves range.
 | Simulation.cpp:1499-1534,1637-1647; ADR-014 §Decision 8
 | What can be targeted
 | Anything with hit points that an enemy owns, a pirate's included. Asteroids, fields and derelicts have no owner and are never targeted.
 | Simulation.cpp:1503-1504; ADR-014 §Decision 10
 | Range
 | Measured between centers, so a big target is no easier to reach
 | Simulation.cpp:121-126; ADR-014 §Decision 7
 | A cold weapon
 | A new ship, or one with no target for a whole interval past its reload, fires its first shot at a random moment within its interval. A ship that loses its target only for a moment keeps its rhythm.
 | Simulation.cpp:607-608,1574-1589; ADR-014 §Decision 5
 | Hits land at the end of the tick
 | Every shot is chosen from where everything stands at the start of the tick, and all the hits land together afterwards. Focus fire can overkill. Then the destroyed (hit points ≤ 0) leave the world, and every order on them ends.
 | Simulation.cpp:1553-1556,1668-1736; ADR-014 §Decision 6
 | The tick's order
 | Fight first, then targets, the economy (work, repair, levels, production, research, mining), movement and separation. A ship that attack-moves stands still while an enemy is in range.
 | Simulation.cpp:940-975; ADR-014 §Decision 9; Simulation.cpp:1873-1875
 | Splash
 | Every other enemy ship or structure whose center is within the splash radius of the target's center takes the same hit, after its own armor. There is no falloff, no friendly fire, and nothing without hit points is hit.
 | Simulation.cpp:1609-1626; ADR-014 §Decision 12
 | Retreat after hits
 | Checked once the tick's hits have landed. A surviving ship whose hit points × 100 fall below max × threshold% turns back to its repairer, unless it is already going.
 | Simulation.cpp:1675-1689; ADR-075 §Decision 5
 | Further station guns
 | A Command Station's guns beyond the first fire at the target the station keeps, each on its own reload
 | Simulation.cpp:1651-1658; ADR-064 §Decision 11
 | Upgrades
 | Change rates and never the size of a hit, armor or a range
 | Tuning.cpp:364-376; ADR-033 §What this forecloses; MVP §8 l.259
Movement as far as it changes play
 | Rule
 | Detail
 | Source
 | Ships turn in arcs, never on the spot
 | Each tick a ship turns toward its next waypoint by at most turn rate × 1/20 s, and moves the way it faces
 | Simulation.cpp:1883-1895,1924; ADR-039 §Decision 1
 | Slowing into a turn
 | speed = cruise × (0.5 + 0.5 × max(0, cos bearing)), where TURN_SPEED_SHARE = 0.5. That is full speed on course and half speed with the waypoint abeam or behind.
 | Simulation.cpp:20-22,1891; ADR-039 §Decision 2
 | Never circling a waypoint
 | speed ≤ turn rate × distance ÷ (2·|sin bearing|)
 | Simulation.cpp:1892-1893; ADR-039 §Decision 3
 | Loop size
 | About twice the half-speed turn radius across: 20 m for Small+Ion, 24 m for Large+Fusion
 | ADR-039 §Decision 2; Phase 1 §9
 | Stall
 | A ship that has come no closer than 0.1 m for 1 s, heading within 45° of its waypoint, gives the waypoint up
 | Simulation.cpp:15-24,1898-1909; ADR-039 §Decision 4; ADR-010 §Decision 6
 | Stop and fire
 | Ships stop dead at their destination and stand to fire on an attack-move. There is no momentum.
 | ADR-039 §Decision 5; Simulation.cpp:1873-1875
 | Groups
 | A group keeps a formation with neighbors 3 footprint radii of its widest ship apart, and arrives together at its slowest ship's pace
 | ADR-010 §Decision 4; DesignStats.h:105-107
 | Ships do not overlap
 | Overlapping ships part. A ship standing to fire gives way only sideways round its target (SIDESTEP_SHARE 0.2).
 | Simulation.cpp:26-28,1928-1934; ADR-010 §Decision 7
 | Attack-order chase
 | A ship on an attack order paths again when its target has moved 40 m, at most once a second
 | Simulation.cpp:62; ADR-014 §Decision 9
 | Banking: presentation only
 | It changes no rule: the simulation, the snapshot and replay are untouched. Small banks up to 50°, full at 60 m/s², settling in 0.25 s. Medium 45°, 26 m/s², 0.3 s. Large 35°, 8 m/s², 0.4 s. Constructor 30°, 55 m/s², 0.3 s.
 | Models.json:60-62,74; ADR-029 §Context, §Decision 4
 | Shot looks: presentation only
 | Hits are instant whatever the look. The Lance's beam is in the side's color and narrows away in 0.25 s. The Rail Cannon's slug is white, 1.5 m wide, with 7 m flashes, fading over 0.4 s. Everything else fires tracers, the Defence gun's included. The looks let a player read the enemy's weapons on sight.
 | Models.json:81-84; ADR-034 §Decision 1-2; ADR-016 §Decision 7; MVP §7 l.202

- A ship one repairer took this tick is not taken by another, so two repairers side by side repair eight (Simulation.cpp:2619-2630; ADR-075 §Decision 3).
- A base repairer repairs any damaged ship of its player in reach, retreating or not, Constructors included (Simulation.cpp:2619 filters EntityKind::Ship, not the warship role) (see Conflicts C10).
- A structure under construction or a pirate structure does not repair (Simulation.cpp:2537-2543).
- Structures are repaired only by Constructors; a Constructor repairs a structure while it is being upgraded (ADR-064 §Decision 4).

The Ore, command points and hit points are derived from Tuning.json with DesignStats.h:86,88, by derive.py. The baseline is the ADR's measurement, not the game's, quoted from ADR-083 §Consequences.
- The setup. No bases, pirates, derelicts or fog of war. Each fleet stands in rows of 5 ships, 60 m apart, its front row the matchup's distance from the other side's. They meet at a node the seed picks, along a line the seed picks (Simulation.cpp:88-91,749-787; ADR-083 §Decision 2).
- No retreat. A matchup's ships never retreat (Simulation.cpp:781-782).
- Research. Matchup designs take the player's research, which is none at the start (Simulation.cpp:774).
- The fleet cap. Matchup ships are placed whole, so the cap does not stop them (ADR-071 §Decision 2).
- The end. A matchup ends when one side has no warship left (FleetDestroyed), or as a draw after MATCHUP_SECONDS = 600 s (TimeLimit) (Simulation.h:348-350; Simulation.cpp:789-804; ADR-083 §Decision 3).
- How it is started. Live it is the game's --matchup <n>, with the owner as player 1. Headless it is OutpostServer --matchups, where the AI's MatchupPlayer plays both sides (ADR-083 §Decision 4-6).
