Orders
Purpose. The seat's scheduled orders, and the form that gives the selection a new one: one trigger, one action and at most one condition (Phase 5 design §7, §11; ADR-080).
Geometry (Hud.cpp:1931-1980). The window is a fixed 640 × 560, so it does not jump as the form changes. Its default place is 1264, 178.

Away
Purpose. What happened while the deputy played the player's seat (Phase 5 design §11).
- Opens itself with the first snapshot that carries the report (GameClient.cpp:539-544).
- Once closed, the report is gone (GameClient.cpp:763-765).
Geometry (Hud.cpp:1983-2007).
- Size. 600 × (36 + 12 + 30 + 22·n + 12), with n ≤ 20 lines (AWAY_LINES = 4 + 16, Hud.h:539).
- Place. Centred on the screen. Example: n = 6 gives 660, 429, 600, 222.
