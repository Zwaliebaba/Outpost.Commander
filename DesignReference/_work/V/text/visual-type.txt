How the faces are made:
- None installed: a face none of whose families is installed throws, and the game stops (GlyphAtlas.cpp:229-246; ADR-030 d2).
- Rasterizing: DirectWrite draws grayscale coverage, NATURAL_SYMMETRIC (GlyphAtlas.cpp:288-290), at max(1, round(units × scale)) px (UiPipeline.cpp:28-31). The atlas is drawn again when the scale moves any face by a whole pixel (ADR-015 d3).
- Characters: printable ASCII plus U+00B7 · and U+00D7 ×. Anything else shows ? (GlyphAtlas.h:62-67).
- Whole pixels: advances, the pen, the top and the tracking are whole pixels (GlyphAtlas.cpp:276; UiPipeline.cpp:270-272).
- Line box: the line box is ceil(ascent + descent + lineGap) from the font's metrics (GlyphAtlas.cpp:258-259). Its value per face is Not specified: it depends on the installed font.

Contrast: text on the panel it sits on
Method, the same as ADR-062 d3 and HudTests.cpp:318-360:
- The ratio is WCAG's (L1 + 0.05) / (L2 + 0.05).
- L = 0.2126R + 0.7152G + 0.0722B, taken straight from the linear constants. They are already linear sRGB, so nothing is decoded.
- Each ground is composited over black at its alpha, in linear.
- A hatched ground is counted twice, once as stripe and once as gap.
- The pairs are those the code draws (use sites in tokens.json). The ratios are computed by A5.
