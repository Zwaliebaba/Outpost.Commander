
Fog, territory and marks
Fog of war
Rule: the client shades a 20 m grid: clear where the player's own entities see now or in a held unsuppressed sector, dim where seen before, dark where never seen; on the ground it is drawn black over the scene, darker for "seen before" than the minimap's (ADR-024, ADR-052, ADR-081).
