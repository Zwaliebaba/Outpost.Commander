 | Value
 | Source

 | the size, the sector grid, the asteroid fields and their passages, the starts, and each home's three asteroids

 | Neuron::Random seeded with the match's seed XOR a constant, so its draws are not the simulation's own

 | Mirroring
 | of each pair of mirrored sectors the one with the lower identifier draws, and each thing it draws is added with its mirror through the center (x, z) → (−x, −z); the center, its own mirror, draws half its count and their mirrors
 | Map.cpp:381-419 (asteroids: lower id draws, center draws half at line 396, both added at 410-412), Map.cpp:437-438 (outposts); ADR-072 §Decision 4; Phase4.md:271
 | Order of draws
 | asteroids, then outposts, then derelicts, then the derelicts' topics (so adding later content changes nothing earlier)
 | Map.cpp:381-507; ADR-072 §Decision 7-8; ADR-074 §Decision 2

 | drawn uniformly inside its sector, borderMeters 250 + its radius in from each edge

 | at least 350 m from its sector's node
 | Map.json: placement.nodeClearanceMeters (line 171); Map.cpp:405; ADR-072 §Decision 5

 | Map.json: placement.oreSpacingMeters (line 171); Map.cpp:367-372, Map.cpp:408; ADR-072 §Decision 5
 | Gap
 | the map's 60 m minimum gap from every obstacle, the edge and every start, for it and its mirror
 | Map.cpp:363-379, Map.cpp:407-408; ADR-072 §Decision 5
 | Attempts
 | up to 10,000 draws per asteroid or derelict; a sector with no room throws, naming the sector
 | Map.cpp:19 (PLACEMENT_ATTEMPTS), Map.cpp:415-416; ADR-072 §Decision 5
 | Outposts
 | per rule, pairs drawn from the free pairs of its kind not taken by an earlier outpost rule; a camp on 2 of the 4 contested pairs; a stronghold on the rich pair and on the center
 | Map.json: outposts (lines 159-163); Map.cpp:420-440; ADR-072 §Decision 7
 | Derelicts
 | per rule, pairs of its kind not taken by an earlier derelict rule (a derelict may share a sector with an outpost); one derelict a sector; placed as an asteroid is but with gap checks only (no ore spacing)
