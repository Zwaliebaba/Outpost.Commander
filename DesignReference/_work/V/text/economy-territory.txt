
Territory, domination and the fleet cap
Territory
The code's names: a sector is held through its node; a Relay (StructureKind::Relay) claims a node; a held sector can be suppressed, cut off, or guarded by pirates (Simulation.cpp:2807-2910).

- A Relay site counts toward the cap; the cap refuses new claims and never takes a node away (Simulation.cpp:2959-2969; ADR-056 §Decision 4; Phase3.md:120-121).

- Ore is paid when the upgrade is ordered; nothing is refunded; the structure keeps working; no Constructor or research shortens a level (Simulation.cpp:2253-2282, 2653-2670; ADR-064 §Decision 2-3).
- Each level adds 20% of the kind's base hit points, added to research's percent (Tuning.json: rules.levelHitPointsPercent 20, line 11; Simulation.cpp:3153-3157; ADR-064 §Decision 4).

- Refusals: UnknownEntity, NotUpgradable, UnderConstruction, AlreadyUpgrading, TopLevel, PrerequisiteMissing (before the Ore), NotEnoughOre (Simulation.cpp:2253-2282; ADR-064 §Decision 2, 10).
