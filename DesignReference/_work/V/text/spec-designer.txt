
Window: Designer
Designer
Purpose. Design a ship, save or rename it, and queue it at a Shipyard, with live figures (Phase 1 design §11, ADR-017, ADR-023, ADR-062, ADR-068 d1, ADR-088 d3). It is drawn after the owner's mockup, GameDesign/Mockups/ShipDesigner.png. The mockup is evidence, and the code differs from it as § Conflicts records.
Height (ExtentOf, Hud.cpp:1075-1092).
- Slot rows start at y 168.
- Each row is lines × card height + (lines − 1) × 7, then 10 to the next row. There are 3 cards to a line, and a card is 80 tall, or 96 in a row where any card has a note.
- The sections start 20 below the last row, and the footer starts at sections + max(26 + 6 × 25, 26 + 96 + 24) + 12.
- The window ends 48 + 20 below the footer.

States.
- A saved design shown. SAVE reads SAVED and is lit in the Queue green; the chip is lit; RENAME is dim.
- New picks. SAVE is enabled; RENAME is dim; QUEUE saves and then queues (ADR-023).
- A saved design renamed. SAVE is dim; RENAME is enabled. With the same name but a changed retreat, the button reads UPDATE (Hud.cpp:606-609).
- Typing. The field's edge is lit, and an _ follows the name. Printable ASCII only, up to 32 characters; Backspace deletes, Enter saves and stops, Esc drops what was typed (Designer.cpp:228-260). A press anywhere but the field stops typing, and so does closing the window (GameClient.cpp:1003-1005, 707-710). While the designer types, every key is its own (GameClient.cpp:819-835).
- Invalid name. The name is in WARNING_COLOR, and SAVE and QUEUE are dim.
- Hovering a part. Hovering an unpicked, unlocked card previews it (Hud.cpp:622-635): the bars show the change in green or red, the values read v (±n), the damage cards show the previewed design with its change, and the hint names the part.
- Locked card. It takes no press and previews nothing (interface plan 1, "Not in this plan").
- No Shipyard. NO SHIPYARD; the arrows are dim; QUEUE is dim; BUILT · 0.
- Hull above the target Shipyard's level. QUEUE is dim and its detail reads Needs Shipyard L{n}; the design can still be saved (Hud.cpp:819-824).
- Queue full. No free slot, so + is dim and QUEUE is dim.
- Ore short. QUEUE is dim.
- More than 3 saved designs. The chip arrows show, each dim at its end.
