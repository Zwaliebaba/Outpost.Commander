
Effects that carry information
Shot looks
Rule: a weapon not listed in Models.json: shots fires tracers; the Lance (2) fires a beam in its shooter's side's color lightened 35% toward white; the Rail Cannon (5) fires a white slug; every hit sparks, a splash weapon throws a ring out to its splash radius (ADR-034, ADR-028 §Decision 6). Effects are opaque flat shapes 6 m above the ground that fade by darkening.
