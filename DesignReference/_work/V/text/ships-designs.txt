
Derived stats and starting designs
How a design's stats are derived
A design is a hull, a drive and a weapon, with a module or none. Its stats are derived from those, never stored in the data (ADR-014 §Decision 1). The server and the designer call the same function, DesignStatsOf, on component views that the server sends with the player's research already applied (ADR-017 §Decision 5; DesignStats.h:75-95; ShipDesign.cpp:38-84).

Designs, starting designs and names
 | Rule
 | Value
 | Source
 | Starting designs
 | Every hull × drive × weapon of components no topic unlocks, in hull, then drive, then weapon order: Small and Medium × Ion × Mass Driver and Lance, four designs
 | ShipDesign.cpp:103-118; ADR-014 §Decision 2
 | Their short names (ADR-069)
 | Swarm: Small+Ion+Mass Driver, S·I·MD, 261 Ore, 30 s. Picket: Small+Ion+Lance, S·I·L, 411 Ore, 30 s. Brawler: Medium+Ion+Mass Driver, M·I·MD, 495 Ore, 60 s. Lancer: Medium+Ion+Lance, M·I·L, 645 Ore, 60 s.
 | Tuning.json: startingDesigns l.114-117; ADR-069 §Decision 1; costs and times derived from DesignStats.h:88-89
 | A starting design's name
 | The short name the data gives it, or else its components' name
 | ShipDesign.cpp:121-130; ADR-069 §Decision 3
 | A design's full name
 | "Hull+Drive+Weapon", then "+Module" when it has one, e.g. "Small+Ion+Mass Driver+Sensor Array". The designer uses the module's initials where the whole name would pass 32 characters.
 | ShipDesign.cpp:132-140; Designer.cpp:122-150; ADR-058 §Decision 6
 | A module on a starting design's components
 | Takes the design's name and the module's initials, "Lancer+SA"
 | Designer.cpp:134-145; ADR-069 §Decision 5
 | Abbreviation
 | The capital initial of each word: hulls S, M, L; drives I, F, P; weapons MD, L, MR, FB, RC; module SA. A design's code is the initials joined by "·", e.g. "S·I·MD·SA".
 | DesignStats.h:23-37; Hud.cpp:420-432; Phase 1 §5 "What the designer shows for each"
 | Name rule
 | 1–32 printable ASCII characters, not all spaces
 | Command.h:68-84; ADR-017 §Decision 6
 | A saved design's components
 | Never change. Other components are a new design, and a rename keeps its components.
 | ADR-017 §Decision 6
 | Queue on unsaved picks
 | Saves the design first, and the queue follows once the server has it. A save not back within 40 ticks (2 s) is dropped.
 | Designer.h:112-121; ADR-023 §Decision 1-4
 | A design's retreat
 | Never, Half (50%) or Quarter (25%); Quarter by default. Every ship built to the design starts with it.
 | RetreatThreshold.h:7-30; ADR-075 §Decision 4
 | Pirates' designs
 | Base-level stats, with no research. Any component will do, since nothing unlocks a pirate's design.
 | Tuning.h:257-258; Tuning.json: pirates l.119-129
 | Matchup designs
 | A starting design where one matches, otherwise saved as "Matchup design N". A matchup's ships never retreat.
 | Simulation.cpp:769-782; ADR-083 §Decision 2
