Lighting
Rule: a face is lit by a warm key from above-behind the default view's top-left (softened by a wrap) plus a fill blended from a cool sky above to a dimmer violet ground below; a line takes only that light's luminance, so it keeps its hue (ADR-090).

Baked occlusion
Rule: the baker stores how open each vertex is (37 cosine-weighted rays within a quarter of the model's extent); a face's light is darkened by how shut in it is, a line's never (ADR-092).

Ships, banking
Rule: a ship is its hull's model in its side's set, centered on its position; it banks into turns in proportion to speed × turn rate, through a critically damped spring, drawn by the client only (ADR-029); it always turns in arcs, never on the spot (ADR-039).
