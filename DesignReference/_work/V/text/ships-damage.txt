Damage after armor
The formula, and how the Designer shows it
- One hit, after armor, in hundredths (DesignStats.h:18-21): HitHundredths(d, a) = max(d / 4, d − a).
- Damage a second against one ship (DesignStats.h:98-103): DamagePerSecond = HitHundredths ÷ 100 ÷ fireIntervalSeconds. The interval is the player's, after research.
- Ships one hit reaches (DesignStats.h:105-129): ShipsReached(splash, r). A formation stands its ships FORMATION_SPACING_RADII = 3 footprint radii apart (DesignStats.h:107; ADR-010 §Decision 4). The function counts the target and every point of a square grid at that spacing whose center lies within the splash. For Small hulls the spacing is 24 m, so the Missile Rack's 30 m and the Flak Battery's 26 m reach 5 ships. For Medium (42 m) and Large (72 m) they reach 1 (ADR-014 §Decision 12).
- What the card shows (DesignStats.h:131-136): FormationDamagePerSecond = DamagePerSecond × ShipsReached.
The Designer's damage section is headed "DAMAGE / S AFTER ARMOR · VS A FORMATION" (Hud.cpp:1510). It has one card per hull, in the snapshot's order (Hud.cpp:781-806, drawn at Hud.cpp:1508-1537). Each card shows:
- the hull's name and "ARM n";
- the figure per ship, as "{:.1f}". The figure is the formation figure. 8.75 is written "8.8" (HudTests.cpp:1718);
- "×5 ships" when a splash reaches more than one;
- "{x.x} / 100 ore", which is the per-ship figure × 100 ÷ the design's cost, module included;
- a bar for the figure's share of the best any design does to that hull. The best is taken over every hull × drive × weapon, locked ones included, at the player's research (Hud.cpp:479-527).
The bar's color gives the rating (Hud.cpp:416-418,534-538; colors Hud.cpp:1001-1004; Phase 1 §11 item 5):
- Good (green) from ⅔ of the best;
- Fair (amber) from ⅓;
- Poor (red) below that.
A hovered part previews the change per ship, e.g. "+4.4" (Hud.cpp:793-800). The production window gives each design three S/M/L marks with the same ratings (Hud.cpp:2884-2891).
The drive and the hull of the firing design do not change the per-ship figure. Only the weapon and the player's fire-rate research do. The design's cost changes the per-Ore figure.
