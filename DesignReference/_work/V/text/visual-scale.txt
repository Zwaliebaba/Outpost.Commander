
Interface scale and the mockup
Interface scale
The steps. Hud::INTERFACE_STEPS are 100, 110, 125, 150, 175 and 200 % (Hud.h:861; ADR-070 d2). The brief's "100/110/125%" matches what a 16:9 screen reaches (see below), not the list of steps. Ctrl+= takes the next step and Ctrl+- the one before (KeyBindings.h:41-42; GameClient.cpp:779-799). The factor starts at 100% (GameClient.h:347), lasts until the game closes, and is never written to disk (ADR-070 d5).
What scales. Hud::Scale = factor × min(width/1920, height/1080) (Hud.cpp:3029-3033). Everything the HUD lays out scales with it, and each anchor is computed in the smaller reference screen (width/scale, height/scale):
- the menu, every HUD panel and every window;
- text, which is rasterized again at the new size (ADR-070 d6, UiPipeline::UseScale GameClient.cpp:577-579);
- hit-testing and window positions (ToUnits, GameClient.cpp:1034-1038).
What does not scale.
- The bars over entities (Hud.cpp:3096; ADR-088 d5).
- The selection ring (ADR-067).
- The stars (GameClient.cpp:1140). ADR-070 d7.
What limits it (Hud::WindowsFit, Hud.cpp:3035-3054; StepInterface, 3056-3073).
- A step up is taken only while these fit the screen less 2 × 16 margins:
 - the Controls window (700 × 762), always;
 - in a match, also the Orders window (640 × 560), the Away window at 20 lines (600 × 530), and the designer laid out for the match's components (818 × 913 with Tuning.json).
- A step down is always taken.
- Re-fit. A new screen size, or a match starting or ending, takes the factor down to the largest step that fits (GameClient.cpp:792-798).

Effects of scaling.
- At 110%, the windows overlap at their default places (ADR-070 Consequences). Research's slot 1 ends at 1070 units, past the designer's default x of 911.
- Layout files: menu-scale-125.json and hud-scale-110.json. In the latter, the selection panel is ≈589.3, 813.8, 323.4, 248.6 and the minimap 17.6, 732.4, 330, 330.
