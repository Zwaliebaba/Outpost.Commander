The persistent world
A world on a dedicated server: seats taken with a token, saves every minute and a log of every command, a deputy while the player is away, and no end.
Sources: Phase5.md A3 A1 ADR-077/078/079/082/084/086

Join → play → lost connection
1The owner runs
The owner runs OutpostServer --new-world <folder> --host <name>, then OutpostServer <folder>. The server writes Join-Player<n>.json (OutpostServer/Main.cpp:136-206).
2The owner hands the player the
The owner hands the player the file. The player puts it at Documents\Outpost Commander\Join.json, or runs --join <file> (ADR-078 §Decision 9).
3The menu shows "Join world" ().
The menu shows "Join world" (Hud.cpp:2332-2333). The player presses it.
4The client connects (10 s handshake)
The client connects (10 s handshake), sends the token, and is seated (10 s for the welcome).
– On failure the menu shows "Could not join the world: …" (WinMain.cpp:349-364, QuicTransport.cpp:19-44).
5The HUD tag reads "Server {host}:{port}"
The HUD tag reads "Server {host}:{port}" (Hud.cpp:3323). If the player was away, the away window opens (§5).
6Snapshots stop.
Snapshots stop.
– After 2 s: "No word from the server · n s" (Hud.cpp:3324-3336).
– A snapshot ends the warning.
7The connection closes
The connection closes, or MsQuic's idle timeout ends it (30 s by default). The client goes back to the menu with "The connection to the server was lost: {reason}" (WinMain.cpp:408-416, 454-466).
8The player joins again with the
The player joins again with the same file, and the newest connection holds the seat (InProcessServer.cpp:305-329).
