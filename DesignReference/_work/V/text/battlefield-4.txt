Exhaust glows
Rule: each exhaust hardpoint makes four additive camera-facing soft glows — a core and three puffs streaming behind — in its drive's color, longer and brighter as the ship's on-screen speed rises to 50 m/s (ADR-019, ADR-034 §Decision 3).

Phosphor glow
Rule: what is bright in the scene (luminance above 0.35, eased over a 0.15 knee) is blurred over five half-resolution levels and added back in the resolve, as a vector screen's phosphor; the interface is drawn after and never glows (ADR-091).
