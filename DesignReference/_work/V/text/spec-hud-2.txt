
HUD: selection, commands, minimap, bars, banner
Selection panel
Purpose. What is selected: its name, its state and its hit points, as figures and bars (ADR-043 d5, ADR-046 d6, ADR-066 d4, ADR-088 d4).
Geometry (Hud.cpp:3357-3426).
- Width. w = clamp(the widest line + 24, 280, 560). Line 0 is measured in the Title face and the others in the Name face; the width is ≈ wherever it falls between the bounds.
- Height. h = 24 + 30 + 22·(lines − 1) + bars. Bars add 8 + rows·10 + (rows − 1)·4 for per-ship bars, or 8 + 6 for the one bar.
- Position. pairLeft = (1920 − w − buttonsPanelWidth)/2, and at least 324 when there is a minimap (Hud.cpp:3369-3371); y = 1064 − h.
- Line 0 is in the Title face, TEXT_COLOR, at (12, 12). Lines 1 and on are in the Name face, NUMBERS_COLOR, at (12, 42 + 22·(i − 1)). Every line is cut short at w − 24.
- Per-ship bars (2 to 24 ships, Hud.h:566): each 17×10, in rows of 12, 4 apart, starting 8 below the lines.
 - Track BAR_TRACK_COLOR. The fill runs from the left by the ship's share of its hit points: HEALTH_GOOD_COLOR above 0.5, HEALTH_FAIR_COLOR above 0.25, otherwise HEALTH_POOR_COLOR (Hud.cpp:1026-1029).
 - A 1-unit outline round a bar: TEXT_COLOR for a retreating ship, HOVER_EDGE_COLOR under the pointer.
 - A click on a bar selects that ship alone and centres the camera on it (Select, Hud.cpp:3399-3417; GameClient.cpp:957-963).
- One bar otherwise: 12, top, w − 24, 6, in the same colors (Hud.cpp:3418-3425).

Commands beside
Purpose.
- A structure's window buttons and its upgrade.
- A warship selection's orders, each with its key.
- A Constructor selection's build buttons.
- The retreat, as three choices.
Sources: ADR-088 d2-d3, ADR-046 d7, Phase 1 design §12.
Geometry (Hud.cpp:3427-3453).
- Panel. w = 380, standing against the selection panel's right edge, bottom-aligned at y 1064.
- Height. h = 24 + n·34 + (n − 1)·6. With a retreat row: (12 + buttons + 6) + 20 + 34 + 12.
- Buttons. Each is 356×34, at (12, 12 + 40·i).
- Retreat row. RETREAT in the Label face, tracking 2, ROW_LABEL_COLOR, at (12, retreatTop). While the ships differ, MIXED stands at the right edge 368, in NUMBERS_COLOR, tracking 2.
- Retreat cells. Three cells of 116×34, 4 apart, at (12 | 132 | 252, retreatTop + 20), reading 25%, 50% and Never in the Name face (Hud.cpp:463-477, 1328-1346).
 - The face is PICKED_COLOR for the setting and CARD_COLOR otherwise; the edge is PICKED_EDGE_COLOR, HOVER_EDGE_COLOR or EDGE_COLOR.
 - While the ships differ, no cell is lit.
 - A press sets that retreat for every selected ship (SetRetreat, GameClient.cpp:936-938).
- Pair position. The pair is centred, and moves right only where it would meet the minimap (ADR-088 d2).

Inside a button (AddButton, Hud.cpp:1264-1323).
- Face: PICKED_COLOR when selected, CARD_COLOR when enabled, FIELD_COLOR when disabled. Only an enabled button takes a press.
- Edge: a 1-unit outline: PICKED_EDGE_COLOR, HOVER_EDGE_COLOR (hovered and enabled), or EDGE_COLOR.
- Label: Name face, TEXT_COLOR (or LOCKED_TEXT_COLOR when disabled), at (+12, +6), cut short only where it would meet the cost, the note or the key cap.
- Cost: the gem and the figure in the Figure face, GOLD_COLOR (or LOCKED_TEXT_COLOR), right edge at w − 12, top at +8.5. The gem is 8×8, with 5 units between it and the figure.
- Key cap: a 20×20 FIELD_COLOR square at (w − 32, +7), outlined in ROW_LABEL_COLOR, with the letter in the Label face in ROW_LABEL_COLOR, centred, at +2.
- Note: Label face, LABEL_COLOR, right edge at w − 12, at +8.5.
- Progress bar: 2, h − 5, w − 4, 3, in BAR_TRACK_COLOR and BAR_FILL_COLOR.

Minimap
Purpose. The whole map: whose sectors are whose, the marks, the fog, the alerts, and the camera's view (ADR-015 d4, ADR-043 d7, ADR-068 d2, ADR-081).
Geometry (Hud.cpp:3456-3465).
- The frame is 16, 764, 300, 300; the map area, MAP_COLOR, is 24, 772, 284, 284.
- Scale. 284 units for Snapshot::mapSizeMeters, 10,000 m with Map.json sizeMeters: 0.0284 units a metre, or 35.2 m a unit.
- Orientation. +x runs right and +z up (Hud.cpp:2432-2451).
Draw order and looks (all in layer 0's panels; the view's segments are lines).
1. Sector wash. Every held or pirate-guarded sector, filled in OWN_COLOR, ENEMY_COLOR or PIRATE_COLOR at alpha 0.12 (Hud.cpp:3481-3485, 42). A sector is 56.8 units square on Map.json's 5×5 grid of 2,000 m sectors.
2. Marks. The neutral ones are drawn first, then the sided ones (Hud.cpp:3486-3528). A mark is a square of side max(least, 2·radius·0.0284). The least sides are: a ship 3, an ore asteroid 8, anything else 6 (Hud.cpp:118-120).

- Fog. A panel over the whole map area, Fill::Fog: black, as opaque as the fog texture's shade at each point (Hud.cpp:3531-3535; drawn GameClient.cpp:1310-1316; ADR-052). Only under fog of war.
- Lattice. Every sector's border, a 1-unit outline in LATTICE_COLOR (Hud.cpp:3567-3568).
- Held and guarded outlines. A 1-unit outline in the side's color at alpha 0.55. It is dashed (6 on, 4 off) while the sector is cut off; while it is suppressed, a hatched fill at alpha 0.35 covers the sector (Hud.cpp:3569-3580).
- Claimable sectors. While a Relay is placed, each sector the player could claim now is outlined in OWN_COLOR, 3 units inside its border (Hud.cpp:3584-3592).
- Remembered structures and derelicts. A cross 8 units across, with arms 2 thick, in the side's color, or DERELICT_COLOR for a neutral one (Hud.cpp:3595-3605).
- Alerts. A 14-unit square outlined 1 unit in WARNING_COLOR (Hud.cpp:3608-3617).
- The camera's view. The four ground corners of the screen, joined by segments 1.5 units wide in VIEW_COLOR, cut at the minimap's edge (Hud.cpp:3621-3629; GameClient.cpp:1040-1054). It is empty when a corner sees past the horizon.
 - At the default 500 m view, unturned, the derived trapezoid is ≈25 units across its far edge, ≈10 across its near edge and ≈14 deep (from Camera.cpp:160-236 and Camera.json).
Interactions.
- A left press on the map area moves the camera there, and dragging keeps moving it (GameClient.cpp:1011-1015, 1026-1031).
- A right press sends the selected ships there (GameClient.cpp:1017-1022).
- The minimap draws only when mapSizeMeters > 0 (Hud.h:611-612).
Bars over entities
ADR-047 d4 and ADR-088 d5.
Geometry (Hud.cpp:3095-3126, 98-101).
- Size. Each bar is 32×5 at the back buffer's scale, not the player's factor. Position and size are whole pixels.
- Stacking. The health bar stands over the build bar, 2 apart, and the pair stands 4 above the "foot". The foot is the top of the model on the screen, in the entity's column (Camera::PixelAbove, GameClient.cpp:731-737).
- Back: the side's color × 0.3 (HEALTH_BACK_SHADE, GameClient.cpp:93-98, 727), or HEALTH_BACK_COLOR for a side the data does not name.
- Health fill: HEALTH_GOOD_COLOR, HEALTH_FAIR_COLOR or HEALTH_POOR_COLOR.
- Build fill: BUILD_BAR_COLOR.
When a bar shows (Hud::BarOver, Hud.cpp:2170-2183).
- The health bar shows while the entity is hurt, or for every entity while Alt is held (KeyBindings.h:38; GameClient.cpp:598).
- The build bar shows while the structure is unfinished.
- Nothing shows for what is only remembered.
- A pair wholly off the screen is left out.
The bars take no click. Example pairs are in hud-busy-warships.json.
