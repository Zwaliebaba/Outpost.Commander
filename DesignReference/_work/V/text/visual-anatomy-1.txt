Window chrome (OpenWindow, Hud.cpp:871-899; AddCorners :852-867; ADR-031 d3)
Anatomy, in units from the window's top-left:
- Frame: the full width × (36 + body), in WINDOW_COLOR, opaque.
- Title bar: (0, 0, width − 36, 36), hatched in TITLE_HATCH_COLOR over the frame. The window is dragged by it.
- Close box: (width − 36, 0, 36, 36) in CLOSE_BOX_COLOR. Its "×" is in the Title face, TEXT_COLOR, at (11, 5) inside the box.
- Corners: four brackets, 12 units each, in WINDOW_EDGE_COLOR.
- No other edge. A window has no outline round its frame.
- The title: OpenWindow can draw a title at (PADDING 12, 5) in Title, but every window passes an empty one (Hud.cpp:1351, 1722, 1823, 1903, 1949, 1993). Each window writes its own label instead: Label face, HEADER_LABEL_COLOR, tracking 2, at (24, 11). The labels are "PRODUCTION", "RESEARCH", "CONTROLS", "SCHEDULED ORDERS", "WHILE YOU WERE AWAY", and the designer's "< SHIPYARD 05 > · DESIGNER".
The header band (HeaderBand, Hud.cpp:1237-1242) is used by the designer, production and research windows:
- It runs the hatching on from y 36 to DESIGNER_HEADER 72.
- It ends in a 1-unit EDGE_COLOR line at y 71.
- The subject is in Title, TEXT_COLOR, at (24, 40).
The Ore box (OreBox, :1244-1249) is 116 × 30 at y 38, ending 24 from the right. It has a FIELD_COLOR face and an EDGE_COLOR outline, with the gem and figure in Title, GOLD_COLOR, right-aligned 10 in.
Stepped subject (Stepped, :1202-1213), "< SHIPYARD 05 >":
- Two arrow buttons stand round the subject, 8 units from it (ARROW_GAP_UNITS).
- In the title bar the arrows are at y 6. On the header's title line they are at y 43 (Hud.cpp:1049-1050).
States:
- A window has no focused or unfocused look. The front window is only drawn last (Hud.cpp:3632-3690). Not specified otherwise.
- There is no hover state on the chrome.
- A window that is dragged off the screen keeps its title bar and 120 units of its width on the screen (Hud.h:646, 852-854).
HUD panel (Frame, Hud.cpp:903-908; ADR-043 d1)
- It is a window's body without a title bar: WINDOW_COLOR and four 12-unit corners in WINDOW_EDGE_COLOR.
- The Ore, status, territory, alerts, hint, connection, banner, selection, buttons, minimap and menu panels are all drawn this way.
- Ore panel (:3141-3156):
- It is 44 tall and as wide as the column.
- The gem and stockpile are in Title, GOLD_COLOR, from x 12.
- The income is in Figure, NUMBERS_COLOR, right-aligned; or, at "+0/s", a warning chip.
- Status line (:3239-3307): the row under the pointer is filled with CARD_COLOR, edge to edge. Plain runs are in Name, TEXT_COLOR. A bar's figure is in Figure, NUMBERS_COLOR.
Warning chip (Painter::Chip, Hud.cpp:1155-1168; ADR-085 d1)
Anatomy:
- The tag is a filled WARNING_COLOR rectangle as wide as the words + 2 × 4 units (CHIP_PAD_UNITS), and as tall as the line: NAME_LINE_UNITS 22, or FIGURE_LINE_UNITS 17 for the income.
- The words are in WINDOW_COLOR, 4 units in, in the line's face (Name, or Figure for the income).
- The tag starts 4 units left of the other lines, so the chip's words line up with theirs (:3208, 3232, 3334).
It marks:
- a status line's IDLE run (:3277-3280)
- an income of nothing (:3152-3155)
- the newest alert (:3227-3232)
- the player's own drain (:3204-3208)
- "No word from the server · N s" (:3334-3338)
States: none. A chip is never cut short (:3272-3279). Contrast is 10.25:1.
Rule: no other filled tag in that color exists (ADR-085 "forecloses").

Damage card (designer, Hud.cpp:1508-1540)
Anatomy:
- FIELD_COLOR with an EDGE_COLOR outline, 96 tall, 8 apart, from x 364.
- The hull is in Name, TEXT_COLOR, at (10, 8). The armor is in Label, LABEL_COLOR, right-aligned 8 in, at y 11.
- The per-ship damage is in LargeFigure at (10, 30). The reach is in Label, LABEL_COLOR, right-aligned at y 28. The change is in Figure, right-aligned at y 44.
- The rating bar is (w − 20) × 4 at (10, 66): BAR_TRACK_COLOR, filled with the rating's color.

States: while a part is hovered, the figure and the change take GOOD_COLOR or POOR_COLOR, or TEXT_COLOR when nothing changes (ChangeColor, :1032-1035).
HUD button (AddButton, Hud.cpp:1264-1323; ADR-043 d4)
Anatomy:
- 34 tall. In the HUD it is 356 wide (380 − 24); on the menu, 416; on the banner, 496.
- The label is in Name at x 12, centered: y = (34 − 22)/2.
- A cost after a "|" in the label is the gem and figure in Figure, right-aligned 12 in, at y = (34 − 17)/2.
- Key cap (:1292-1301):
- 20 × 20, right-aligned 12 in, centered;
- FIELD_COLOR face and ROW_LABEL_COLOR outline;
- its letter in Label, ROW_LABEL_COLOR, at y +2.
- Progress bar: inset 2, 3 tall, along the foot. BAR_TRACK_COLOR, filled with BAR_FILL_COLOR.
- A label is cut short with "..." only where it would meet the cost, the note or the key cap.
