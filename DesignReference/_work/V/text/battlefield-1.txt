Frame and scene target
Rule: the scene is drawn at the display's native resolution into a 4-sample sRGB target, resolved by a shader into the back buffer, then the interface is drawn over it; there is no HDR.
 | Property
 | Value
 | Source

 | Depth
 | 32-bit float, cleared to 1, less-than; back faces culled

 | WinMain.cpp:21; Renderer.cpp:24
Example: a crisp, antialiased image filling the monitor, pure black behind everything.

Starfield
Rule: one fixed-seed sky of 50,000 stars at infinity, spread evenly over the sphere with a natural brightness distribution; the 25 brightest are crosses, the rest Gaussian points; it never moves when the view pans, only when it turns (ADR-022, ADR-028).
