Hardpoints
A hardpoint is a glTF empty named hp_<tag>. The game knows two tags: gun, where a shot leaves from, and exhaust (ADR-018 §Decision 4; Hardpoints.h:5-16; Hardpoints.cpp:9-10,45-50).
Hardpoints are presentation only. A shot leaves from the gun nearest its target, and gun n of a Command Station from the n-th nearest, wrapping round. Range is still measured from the ship's center (Hardpoints.h:68-73; Hardpoints.cpp:205-220).

Model sets and colors (Models.json):
- Human is blue (0.18, 0.42, 0.95), its own meshes (l.4-17).
- Tarkan is orange-red (0.9, 0.25, 0.12), its own meshes (l.19-31).
- Pirate is purple (0.62, 0.22, 0.9) on Tarkan meshes (l.34-36).
- Wreck is grey (0.4, 0.4, 0.42) on Human meshes (l.39-41).
- Player 1 plays Human and player 2 Tarkan (l.53-56).
- The Relay is drawn with the ResearchLab model at tint 1.6, and the Repair Bay with the Shipyard model at tint 0.6 (l.70-71).
- Mesh lengths: hulls 20, 35 and 60 m; Constructor 24; CommandStation 60; Shipyard 60; ResearchLab 12; MiningRig 4; DefensePlatform 4 (l.7-15).
- CommandStation, Shipyard and ResearchLab have "levels": 5 (l.11-13).
