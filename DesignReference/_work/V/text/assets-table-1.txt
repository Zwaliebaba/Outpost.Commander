Triangles are indexCount / 3 of the .nmf; vertices its vertexCount. Bounds in mesh units are the raw .nmf min → max (x front, y up, z left: Tools/BakeMeshes.py docstring, Hardpoints.h:20); fitted bounds in meters are after FitMesh/FitMeshAcross (centered, so ± half the extent). Drawn in game is the size on the map: a ship at its fitted size (ModelPose.scale = 1, Hardpoints.h:28); a structure scaled by 2 × footprintRadiusMeters / lengthMeters (GameClient.cpp:1661-1664, radius from Tuning.json via Simulation.cpp:1069); a derelict by 2 × radius / lengthMeters of that derelict; a rock by its radius (2 m mesh). Crease lines are this tool's replay of BuildCreaseLines at 10°, summed over pieces.
