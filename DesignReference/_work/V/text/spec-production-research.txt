
Windows: Production and Research
Production
Purpose. One producer's queue, and the cards that add to it: a Constructor at the Command Station, or each saved design at a Shipyard. Arrows step through the player's finished producers (Phase 1 design §12; owner, 2026-10-03; ADR-043 d6; ADR-068 d3).
Geometry (Hud.cpp:1606-1636, 1708-1784).
- The window is 478 wide (PRODUCTION_WINDOW_WIDTH = 1920 − 64 − 818 − 560).
- Cards are 211 × 74, two to a line, at x 24 or 243, y 104 + 82·line.
- The cards stay where they are as the queue grows; the window grows at its foot.
- Height: CardsBottom + 20 + 20 + jobs·34 + 20, where CardsBottom = 104 + max(lines, 1)·82 − 8.

States.
- Command Station. One Constructor card; no strengths.
- Shipyard. A card for each saved design. A design whose hull is above the Shipyard's level is dim and says NEEDS L{n}.
- No producer. NO PRODUCER and its hint.
- Shipyard with no designs. Its hint.
- Queue full (5 / 5). Every card is dim.
- Front job. It shows progress, WAITING FOR ORE, or WAITING FOR FLEET CAP.
Layout files: window-production-shipyard.json, -station.json, -none.json.
Research
Purpose. The player's Research Lab, its queue, and each topic not yet researched or queued, tier by tier. A topic that needs something is hatched and says what it needs (Phase 1 design §12, Phase 3 design §6, ADR-061 d4, ADR-068 d1).
Geometry (Hud.cpp:1786-1884).
- The window is 560 wide. Topic cards are 252 wide, two to a line, at x 24 or 284, y 104 + line·(TH + 8). It shows 10 topics, scrolling by a row (TOPIC_COLUMNS, TOPICS_SHOWN, Hud.h:400-401; StepTopics, Hud.cpp:2991-2999).
- Card height. TH = 28 + lines·18 + 4, where lines is the most any topic needs: its effect's wrapped lines plus max(1, its needs). It is at least 2.
- With Tuning.json, TH = 86. "Constructor build and repair rate +25%" wraps to 2 lines in the Detail face, and with its need, "RESEARCH LAB L3", that makes 3.
- Height. CardsBottom + 20 + 20 + jobs·34 + 20. For page 1 with two jobs: 510, 178, 560, 694.
