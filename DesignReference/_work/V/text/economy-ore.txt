These are Phase 3's 5, 6, 8 and 10 lowered by about a third (owner, 2026-10-07): Phase4.md:216, Phase4.md:203; ADR-036 §Decision 3.
A rig's income each second = its yield's rate × its owner's mining-income factor × (20% once its asteroid is dry, else 1) × its sector's share (0 outside a held sector or in a suppressed one, 50% if cut off, else 1), rounded to the nearest hundredth. Simulation.cpp:2782-2793 (RigIncomeHundredthsPerSecond), Simulation.cpp:2971-2979 (TerritoryShare).
- Mining-income factor: Improved Extraction, +25% (Tuning.json: research[id 1], lines 66-67). Percentages of one stat add before becoming a factor (Research.cpp:52, Research.cpp:110-117; ADR-033 per the comment).
