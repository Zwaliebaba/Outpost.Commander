## Pitch and pillars (owner's words)

The design documents are the owner's (`MVP.md:3`: "Owner: Stefan Zwaal").

**The pitch** (`MVP.md:9`):

> Outpost Commander is a real-time strategy game in space, built on the model of **Warzone 2100**: build a base with constructor units on contested resource points, research new technology, **design your own ships from components**, and use them to destroy the enemy.

**The MVP's question** (`MVP.md:11`): "The MVP exists to answer one question — **is this game worth building?**"

**The pillars** (`MVP.md:27-32`, verbatim):

1. **Your fleet is your design.** Ships are assembled from a hull, a drive and a weapon. The best design depends on what the enemy fields, and changing it mid-match is a real decision. `MVP.md:29`
2. **Territory is income.** Ore comes from asteroids you hold. Expanding, defending and raiding mining rigs is the strategic layer. `MVP.md:30`
3. **Research is recovery.** Tech unlocks new components and upgrades. What you research determines which designs you can build. `MVP.md:31`
4. **Readable at a glance.** A flat battlefield under an RTS camera. You can always see who is where and who is winning. `MVP.md:32`

**Premise** — marked "proposal — edit freely" (`MVP.md:15`): "A century ago the jump relays went dark. The colonies on the far side — the **Terrakin** …" and "The **Kessler Reach** is an asteroid belt thick with ore and the wrecks of that fleet, and whoever holds it can rebuild. … The player is an **Outpost Commander**: land a Command Station, mine the rocks, recover lost technology, and design the ships that will keep what you have taken." (`MVP.md:17-19`). The opponent faction is "**The Tarkan High Command**, the Tarkan for short" (`MVP.md:23`).

**What each later phase asked, in the owner's words** (each a quoted owner question):

| Phase | The owner's question | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | "**can a match last long enough to be a campaign of its own, and still be the same game?**" | `Phase1.md:13` |
| 2 | "**today a match is two players building ships and, in the end, one siege of the enemy's base. There are no real skirmishes along the way.**" | `Phase2.md:11` |
| 3 | "**a Shipyard is upgraded before it builds bigger ships, a Command Station is upgraded to control more sectors, and a Research Lab is upgraded before it researches further.**" (owner's direction) | `Phase3.md:11` |
| 4 | "**a match is a swarm from the first minutes. A ship should take more effort to build, so that the opening is spent finding out what is around: mining, salvage and small fights. The universe has to be much bigger for that.**" | `Phase4.md:11` |
| 5 | "**does a world that keeps running while its player is away work at all?**" | `Phase5.md:11` |

Phase 5's one-paragraph description of a world (`Phase5.md:13`): "A world is today's match that does not stop. It runs on a server the owner keeps going, on Phase 4's 10 km system, with two seats … While the player is away, a deputy keeps the player's empire running, and orders the player gave in advance carry on. The server saves the world every minute and comes back from a restart with what it had."

The loop the MVP tested, in its own words: "Is the loop — mine, build, research, design, fight — fun?" (`MVP.md:40`, Q1).

## Loops

### Match loop (player level)

1. **Setup.** The map's content is placed from the match's seed: asteroids, then pirate outposts, then derelicts, mirrored through the center. `InProcessServer.cpp:133`; `Map.cpp:354-508`; `ADR-072 §Decision 3`.
2. **Players added** with 1,000 Ore and the four starting designs saved; fog on; pirates and derelicts spawned. `InProcessServer.cpp:150-167`; `Tuning.json: rules.startingOre`, `startingDesigns`.
3. **Bases placed:** each player's Command Station on its start (holding its home sector from the first tick) and 2 Constructors; 1,000 tickets each. `Simulation.cpp:688-727`; `ADR-056 §Decision 3`; `ADR-057 §Decision 4`.
4. **Mine:** Constructors build Mining Rigs (50 Ore) on the three home asteroids; income flows each tick. `Tuning.json` line 60; `Simulation.cpp:3003-3027`; `Phase2.md:69`.
5. **Claim:** Relays (200 Ore) on the two flank nodes open the near asteroids; further claims grow along the lattice, up to the Command Station's node cap. `Phase2.md:53`, `Phase2.md:69`; `Simulation.cpp:2134-2140`, `2170-2171`.
6. **Build the base:** Shipyard (in a held sector), Research Lab (one), Defence Platforms, Repair Bay; costs paid at the start, nothing refunded. `Simulation.cpp:2145-2185`; `MVP.md:150`.
7. **Explore and salvage:** scouts find derelicts and pirate outposts; Constructors salvage derelicts for Ore and research; fleets clear outposts to open guarded sectors. `Phase4.md:21`; `ADR-074 §Decision 4-5`; `ADR-073 §Decision 6`.
8. **Research and grow:** research topics within the Lab's open tier; upgrade the Command Station (nodes, fleet cap, guns), the Shipyard (hulls) and the Lab (tiers, second slot). `ADR-064 §Decision 9-11`; `Tuning.json` lines 40-58.
9. **Design and produce:** warships from saved designs, each job waiting for Ore and for room under the fleet cap. `Simulation.cpp:2674-2762`; `ADR-071 §Decision 3`.
10. **Fight:** suppress and destroy enemy Relays, defend held sectors; damaged ships retreat at their threshold and are repaired at a Repair Bay, the Command Station or a Shipyard. `ADR-056 §Decision 6`; `ADR-075 §Decision 5`.
11. **Ore runs out:** home rigs run dry and pay the 20% trickle; income moves outward. `ADR-035 §Decision 2`; `Phase1.md:206`.
12. **Domination drains** every 3 s by the node difference; a player out of tickets loses. `Simulation.cpp:3778-3805`.
13. **Losing production** (no Command Station and no finished Shipyard) loses the match; a station-less player's Shipyards are revealed. `Simulation.cpp:3683-3710`, `316-330`.
14. **End:** Victory, Defeat or Draw banner with the match's length ("By domination." when so); the world runs on until the player goes back to the menu. `Hud.cpp:2199-2207`; `ADR-020 §Decision 9`.

### Tick loop (one simulation tick, code order)

1. Planned large orders of the last tick finish; this tick's commands are applied in order. `Simulation.cpp:931-938`
2. `Fight` — ships and armed structures fire; hits land; retreats start; the destroyed leave; cleared outposts leave wrecks. `Simulation.cpp:944-947`, `1557-1738`
3. `DecideMatch` (loss of production, or a world's restarts), `KeepRetreats`, `KeepStandingOrders`, `GuardOutposts`, `ChaseTargets`, `ApproachWork`. `Simulation.cpp:948-956`
4. Economy: `Work` (build, repair, salvage), `RepairShips`, `BuildLevels`, `Produce`, `Research`, `Mine`. `Simulation.cpp:957-966`
5. `MoveShips`, then `SeparateShips`, `KeepShipsClear`. `Simulation.cpp:967-975`
6. `UpdateTerritory`, then `Dominate`. `Simulation.cpp:978-979`
7. `UpdateVision` (under fog), `RaiseSightings`, `FireScheduledOrders`; a quiet tick builds one dropped path graph; the tick count advances. `Simulation.cpp:980-994`

### World loop

1. **Make a world:** `OutpostServer --new-world <folder>` makes one with a seed and the seats; `OutpostServer <folder>` runs it. A new world gets the world's rules (no domination, restarts). `Phase5.md:64`; `InProcessServer.cpp:86-90`.
2. **Recover on start:** load the newest save that reads whole, then apply the logged commands since it, tick by tick. `Phase5.md:84`; `WorldFolder.cpp:104-130`; `InProcessServer.cpp:78-83`.
3. **Tick:** 20 a second; each tick's commands are written to the world's command log before they are applied. `Tuning.json: rules.tickHz`; `InProcessServer.cpp:477-482`; `Phase5.md:83`.
4. **Save:** every 60 s of ticks; the newest 3 saves kept; written to a new file and renamed into place. `WorldFolder.h:15` (`WORLD_SAVE_SECONDS = 60`), `WorldFolder.h:23` (`SAVES_KEPT = 3`); `InProcessServer.cpp:484-485`; `Phase5.md:82`.
5. **Seats:** a seat nobody has taken yet is its deputy's; a player takes its seat with its token and plays as in a match. `SeatController.cpp:9-12`; `Phase5.md:67`, `Phase5.md:95`.
6. **Away:** 60 s after the player's connection drops, the deputy (a keeper) takes the seat; scheduled orders fire on their ticks. `GameProtocol/Server.h:32` (`DEPUTY_DELAY_SECONDS = 60`); `SeatController.cpp:13-21`; `Phase5.md:47`, `Phase5.md:95-96`, `Phase5.md:107`.
7. **Return:** the player takes the seat again; the deputy gives it back; a panel says what happened while it was away; what the deputy ordered stands. `Phase5.md:95`, `Phase5.md:155`; `InProcessServer.cpp:521-531`.
8. **Loss and restart:** a player without a Command Station or a finished Shipyard is told; an hour of ticks later, once its start is free, it restarts with a Command Station, 2 Constructors and its Ore topped up to 1,000. `Simulation.cpp:3715-3748`; `Simulation.h:343`.
9. **Hourly record:** the world log writes each seat's Ore, income, fleet against its cap, nodes, topics researched, and time present and deputy-played, every hour of ticks. `WorldLog.cpp:101-115`; `Phase5.md:137`, `Phase5.md:143`.
10. **Outage:** while the server is down the world pauses; on start it recovers (step 2). `Phase5.md:85`.
11. **End:** a world ends only when the owner stops it. `Phase5.md:127`.
