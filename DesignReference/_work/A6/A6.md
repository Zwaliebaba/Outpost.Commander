# A6 — Asset renders: every shipped model in an approximation of the game's look

Agent **A6**. HEAD `a053fe294d91dc885bbb061c2f34d76ca6897738` (2026-10-25T08:33:55+01:00). Read-only on the repository; everything below is under `DesignReference/_work/` (images in `DesignReference/_work/assets/`, the tool in `DesignReference/_work/A6/tool/`, data in `DesignReference/_work/A6/*.json`).

**Every model image here, and every sheet, is an approximation of the in-game look**: rendered offline from the shipped meshes with the game's numbers, not captured from the game, which was not built or run (no Windows, MSBuild, GPU or shader compiler in this container). Each image also says so in its bottom-left corner.

**Deviation from the brief, recorded:** the brief says to render every GLB in `OutpostCommander/Assets/Models/`, but that folder holds the baked `.nmf` files the game loads (45), and the glTF sources are in `Art/Models/` (45). The renders are made from the `.nmf` files: they are what ships, and only they carry the baked occlusion (ADR-092). The GLBs are used for hashes and the bake check, which proves each `.nmf` is exactly what its GLB bakes to (below).

## Renderer and how it replicates the game's shading

**Renderer:** a software rasterizer in Python 3.13 and numpy 2.5 (`DesignReference/_work/A6/tool/nmfrender.py`, driven by `render_all.py`), not three.js: z-buffered triangles, then a depth-tested line pass, then the glow, all in linear color, encoded to sRGB at the end. No Blender, no GPU. It is the same arithmetic as the shaders, replayed per pixel.

**Where this is not the game, on purpose or of necessity:**

- **Lines are 2 px wide at 1024 px** (about 1 px in the `_512` copies). The game draws 1-pixel lines at any zoom (ADR-027 decision 3), but a model fills ~880 px here against ~80 px for a 20 m hull at the default zoom (500 m across 1920 px), so 1 px would read far thinner than the game does.
- **Each image is framed by its own projected extent** (the model fills 84 % of the side), so images are **not to scale** with each other. The level sheets are the exception: one frame for all five levels.
- **Not drawn:** the starfield and grid behind the model, banking, exhaust glows at `exhaust` hardpoints (ADR-019), footprint rings (ADR-042), selection, health bars, a structure under construction (darker by `UNBUILT_SHADE`), a Mining Rig standing on its rock (ADR-044), a remembered structure's lines-only look (`MEMORY_GRAY_SHARE`, `MEMORY_SHADE`, `GameClient.cpp:119-120`). Spinning parts are drawn at rest (angle 0), as the baker and the explosion take them (ADR-045 decisions 2 and 5).
- **Not built, so not drawn** (`ImplementationPlan-Look.md` task board): paint slots, two-tone lines, beacons, fleet plates (LV2.2, LV2.3, LV3.1, LV4.1–4.3).
- **Transparent background** (straight alpha, written so that it shows as rendered over black in an ordinary viewer): the sky is not there, and a model's dark faces read lighter on a white page than on the game's black.

- **Asteroids**: Small, Medium, Large, in the ore-asteroid look.

## Duplicates

- **No two files are byte-identical**: sha256 over the 45 `.nmf` and the 45 `.glb` files gives 90 distinct hashes (the table above). The Tarkan `CommandStation_L1`–`L3` GLBs are all 17,376 bytes, and differ.
- **Pirate and Wreck have no files of their own**: they read Tarkan's and Human's folders by name (`Models.json` `"meshes"`; `ModelFileName`, `ModelCatalog.cpp:362-369`; ADR-011 decision 4: "the pirates' meshes are loaded a second time, in their own color"). So `Pirate/<Model>` renders are Tarkan's meshes, and `Wreck/<Hull>` Human's.
- **Human and Tarkan share nothing**: every same-named pair differs in triangle count (table above).
- **Levels that share a topology** (same index buffer, some vertices moved), measured by comparing the `.nmf` arrays: Human `CommandStation_L1`/`L2`/`L3` (1,560 vertices; L2 and L3 longer along x by ×1.106 and ×1.213); Tarkan `CommandStation_L1`/`L2`/`L3` (132 of 654 vertices move) and `L4`/`L5` (132 of 708); Tarkan `ResearchLab_L1`/`L2` (24 of 720) and `L3`/`L4` (212 of 1,008); Tarkan `Shipyard_L4`/`L5` (624 of 1,404). Each level is still a different shape, and its occlusion differs.
- **Art ↔ Assets**: 45 GLBs, 45 NMFs, one to one by set folder and name, all current by `--check` (above).

## Open / unclear

- **The brief's GLB path.** `OutpostCommander/Assets/Models/` holds `.nmf` files, not GLBs; rendered from the `.nmf` (above).
- **Wreck renders only the hulls.** `Models.json` gives the Wreck set all 21 Human meshes (and the game loads them all), but only the three hull models are ever drawn as derelicts (`GameClient.cpp:1628-1639`). If the lead wants all 21 in gray, `render_all.py` drops them at `entries()` (one condition).
- **Pirate structures other than the Defense Platform** are rendered because the data loads them, but nothing in `GameLogic/` spawns them (`Simulation.cpp:843` spawns only Defense Platforms for `PIRATES`). Whether they can ever appear is A1/A2's to settle.
- **The look is approximate**: line width (2 px against 1 px), orthographic against a 45° perspective, 2×2 supersampling against 4× MSAA, and the glow replayed on a catalogue-sized image rather than at the game's screen scale, so it reads stronger relative to the model than in the game at the default zoom. No in-game capture exists to compare against; the owner's run is the reference.
- **Field rocks** are darker than an ore asteroid (`FIELD_SHADE` 0.7, `GameClient.cpp:1522`); only the ore-asteroid look is rendered.
- **Mining Rig** is rendered alone; in the game it stands tilted on its rock's surface (ADR-044, `RigStance`, `GameClient.cpp:1718-`).
- **Structures are drawn larger than `lengthMeters`.** A structure is drawn across its footprint, `2 × footprintRadiusMeters` (`GameClient.cpp:1661-1664`), so e.g. a Command Station L1 is 90 m long on the map against `lengthMeters` 60, a Mining Rig 50 m against 4, a Research Lab 60 m against 12. The *Drawn in game* column gives each; ADR-011 decision 2 calls `lengthMeters` how big a model is, which holds for ships but not for structures. Recorded for A2/A5, not as a conflict: the code is explicit.
- **Logos are placeholders** (above): if the reference shows a logo, there is none to show.
- **Three of the five textures are referenced by no code** (`glow`, `cloudyglow`, `laser`), and `starburst` only by a test; they are in the package all the same.
