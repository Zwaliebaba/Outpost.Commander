## How a design's stats are derived (ADR-014, ADR-017)

A design is a hull, a drive and a weapon, with a module or none. Its stats are derived from those, never stored in the data (`ADR-014 §Decision 1`). The server and the designer call the same function, `DesignStatsOf`, on component views that the server sends with the player's research already applied (`ADR-017 §Decision 5`; `DesignStats.h:75-95`; `ShipDesign.cpp:38-84`).

| Stat | Formula | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Hull view hit points | `llround(hull.hitPoints × 100 × hullHitPointsFactor)` hundredths. Research applies before the drive. | `ShipDesign.cpp:42` |
| Design hit points | `llround(hullView.hp × drive.hitPointsFactor)` hundredths | `DesignStats.h:86` |
| Armor | `hull.armor × 100` hundredths. Nothing changes it. | `ShipDesign.cpp:43`; `DesignStats.h:87` |
| Speed | hull speed × shipSpeedFactor (research) × drive.speedFactor × module.speedFactor (1.0 without a module) | `ShipDesign.cpp:44`; `DesignStats.h:81-82` |
| Turn rate | hull turn rate × drive.turnRateFactor. Research never changes it. | `DesignStats.h:83-84`; `ADR-033 §Decision 4` |
| Footprint | the hull's | `DesignStats.h:85` |
| Cost | hull + drive + weapon + module, in whole Ore | `DesignStats.h:88` |
| Build time | the hull's buildSeconds. A Shipyard's job advances by `shipyardBuildSpeedFactor` each tick. | `DesignStats.h:89`; `Simulation.cpp:2737-2741` |
| Damage, interval, range, splash | the weapon's, with the interval divided by that weapon's fire-rate factor | `ShipDesign.cpp:64-74`; `DesignStats.h:90-93` |
| Module sight | the module's sightMeters, or 0 | `DesignStats.h:94` |
| Research factors | `1 + (sum of the percents on that rate) ÷ 100`. Percents on one rate add (+15% and +15% make +30%). Fire-rate percents are summed per weapon. | `Research.cpp:50-125` (factor at l.110); `ADR-033 §Decision 3` |
| Research landing | When a topic finishes, every design of the player's is re-derived. Each warship keeps its share of its hit points, rounded to the nearest hundredth and never below 1, and takes its design's speed. | `Simulation.cpp:3105-3151`; `ADR-017 §Decision 2` |

## Damage after armor

### The formula, and how the Designer shows it

- **One hit, after armor, in hundredths** (`DesignStats.h:18-21`): `HitHundredths(d, a) = max(d / 4, d − a)`.
- **Damage a second against one ship** (`DesignStats.h:98-103`): `DamagePerSecond = HitHundredths ÷ 100 ÷ fireIntervalSeconds`. The interval is the player's, after research.
- **Ships one hit reaches** (`DesignStats.h:105-129`): `ShipsReached(splash, r)`. A formation stands its ships `FORMATION_SPACING_RADII = 3` footprint radii apart (`DesignStats.h:107`; `ADR-010 §Decision 4`). The function counts the target and every point of a square grid at that spacing whose center lies within the splash. For Small hulls the spacing is 24 m, so the Missile Rack's 30 m and the Flak Battery's 26 m reach **5** ships. For Medium (42 m) and Large (72 m) they reach **1** (`ADR-014 §Decision 12`).
- **What the card shows** (`DesignStats.h:131-136`): `FormationDamagePerSecond = DamagePerSecond × ShipsReached`.

**The Designer's damage section** is headed "DAMAGE / S AFTER ARMOR · VS A FORMATION" (`Hud.cpp:1510`). It has one card per hull, in the snapshot's order (`Hud.cpp:781-806`, drawn at `Hud.cpp:1508-1537`). Each card shows:

- the hull's name and "ARM n";
- the figure per ship, as `"{:.1f}"`. The figure is the formation figure. 8.75 is written "8.8" (`HudTests.cpp:1718`);
- "×5 ships" when a splash reaches more than one;
- "{x.x} / 100 ore", which is the per-ship figure × 100 ÷ the design's cost, module included;
- a bar for the figure's share of the best any design does to that hull. The best is taken over every hull × drive × weapon, locked ones included, at the player's research (`Hud.cpp:479-527`).

The bar's color gives the rating (`Hud.cpp:416-418,534-538`; colors `Hud.cpp:1001-1004`; Phase 1 §11 item 5):

- **Good** (green) from ⅔ of the best;
- **Fair** (amber) from ⅓;
- **Poor** (red) below that.

A hovered part previews the change per ship, e.g. "+4.4" (`Hud.cpp:793-800`). The production window gives each design three S/M/L marks with the same ratings (`Hud.cpp:2884-2891`).

## Structures

Common rules:

- **The base numbers** come from `Tuning.json: structures`, lines 40–63.
- **Hit points at level L** are base × (research factor + 20% × (L − 1)), with `levelHitPointsPercent` 20 (`Tuning.json:11`; `Simulation.cpp:3153-3157`; `ADR-064 §Decision 4`). The table also gives each level with Reinforced Structures (+25%, `Tuning.json:90-91`).
- **Levels.** Only the Command Station, the Shipyard and the Research Lab have levels, up to `MAXIMUM_STRUCTURE_LEVEL` 5 (`Tuning.h:155-156`; `Tuning.cpp:315-325`). A kind's top level is 1 + the length of its `levels` list (`Tuning.h:185-188`).
- **An upgrade** is paid when it is ordered and builds itself over the level's `buildSeconds`. No Constructor and no research shortens it, and the structure keeps working (`Simulation.cpp:2253-2282,2650-2668`; `ADR-064 §Decision 2-3`).
- **A level lands whole.** The structure keeps its share of its hit points (`ADR-064 §Decision 4`).
- **A destroyed structure** is rebuilt at L1 (`ADR-064 §Decision 5`).
- **Building.** A Constructor builds a structure, which is paid in full when the order is given. The site starts at 1/10 of its hit points and rises with the work (`Simulation.cpp:2145-2185,2449-2465`; `ADR-016 §Decision 4-5`).
- **Build time with n Constructors** is `buildConstructorSeconds ÷ (1 + 0.5 × (n − 1)) ÷ constructor-rate factor` (`Simulation.cpp:2436-2437`).
- **Obstacles.** Every structure but the Mining Rig blocks movement (`ADR-016 §Decision 8`).
- **Sight.** An unarmed structure sees 200 m and an armed one 300 m (`Simulation.cpp:252-262`).
- **The panel's next-level line** reads "L{n}: {gives}{hit points} hit points" (`Hud.cpp:2645-2699`).

### Retreat (all ships)

- **The trigger.** A hit that leaves a ship below its threshold, Half (50%) or Quarter (25%), sends it to its repairer. The repairer is the nearest Repair Bay, else the Command Station, else the nearest Shipyard. With none, the ship fights on (`Simulation.cpp:2554-2589`; `ADR-075 §Decision 5`).
- **Where it stops.** 40 m beyond the repairer's footprint (`Simulation.cpp:86`).
- **The end.** The retreat ends when the ship is whole, or when its player gives it an order (`ADR-075 §Decision 6`).

**Rules.**

- Tier 1 is open from the start. A Research Lab at L2 opens tier 2, and one at L3 opens tier 3 (`ADR-033 §Decision 2`; `Tuning.cpp:703-712`).
- A topic is refused while its tier is not open (`LevelTooLow`, `Simulation.cpp:3190-3192`), or while a prerequisite is neither researched nor queued ahead of it (`Simulation.cpp:3181-3189`).
- A topic is paid when it starts (`Simulation.cpp:3062-3067`).
- An upgrade applies at once to every existing ship and structure of the player (`Simulation.cpp:3105-3141`).
- Percents on one rate add (`ADR-033 §Decision 3`).
- The research window lists topics tier by tier. A topic not yet open is dim, and its first need reads "RESEARCH LAB L2" or "RESEARCH LAB L3" (`Hud.cpp:2969-2986`). Each card reads "TIER n · s" (`Hud.cpp:2978`).
- The effect texts below are `EffectText`'s (`Research.cpp:142-179`).

Topic IDs 9 and 18, the gateways Relay Archives and Precursor Vault, are gone and their IDs are not reused (`ADR-033 §Decision 2`). "req" names a topic's prerequisites by ID; each entry gives its Ore and its research time. Every line's source is `Tuning.json: research[i]`, lines 66–111.

**What each kind of upgrade applies to** (`Research.h:5-28`; `ADR-033 §Decision 4`):

- **Hull hit points** go on all hulls, before the drive's factor. They do not reach the Constructor or structures.
- **Fire rate** divides that weapon's interval, from its next shot; a reload under way is not shortened.
- **Structure hit points** go on every structure, whose standing ones keep their share.
- **The Defence gun's fire rate** divides its interval when it fires.
- **Ship speed** goes on every warship, through its hull speed, and on the Constructor. Turn rates are unchanged, and a move under way keeps its pace.
- **The Constructor's rate** raises both its build work and its repair.
- **Shipyard build speed** speeds Shipyard jobs only, not the Command Station's.
- **Mining income and ore reserve** are economy; see A1.

- **The setup.** No bases, pirates, derelicts or fog of war. Each fleet stands in rows of 5 ships, 60 m apart, its front row the matchup's distance from the other side's. They meet at a node the seed picks, along a line the seed picks (`Simulation.cpp:88-91,749-787`; `ADR-083 §Decision 2`).
- **No retreat.** A matchup's ships never retreat (`Simulation.cpp:781-782`).
- **Research.** Matchup designs take the player's research, which is none at the start (`Simulation.cpp:774`).
- **The fleet cap.** Matchup ships are placed whole, so the cap does not stop them (`ADR-071 §Decision 2`).
- **The end.** A matchup ends when one side has no warship left (`FleetDestroyed`), or as a draw after `MATCHUP_SECONDS` = 600 s (`TimeLimit`) (`Simulation.h:348-350`; `Simulation.cpp:789-804`; `ADR-083 §Decision 3`).
- **How it is started.** Live it is the game's `--matchup <n>`, with the owner as player 1. Headless it is `OutpostServer --matchups`, where the AI's `MatchupPlayer` plays both sides (`ADR-083 §Decision 4-6`).

## Open / unclear

1. **Shipyard L4–L5 and Research Lab L5 are drawn but not built.** The art has 5 levels for both (`Models.json:12-13`; `ADR-045 §Decision 1`), but `Tuning.json` stops the Shipyard at L3 (l.50-53) and the Lab at L4 (l.55-59). Their cost, time and effect are **Not specified**: those levels cannot be reached. Phase 3 §5 and §6 say so on purpose (gate K3).
2. **The Mining Rig mesh carries a `gun` hardpoint** (`Art/Models/*/MiningRig.glb`), but the rig has no weapon (`Tuning.json:60`). Nothing fires from it. `ADR-018 §Consequences` speaks only of "a gun on the two armed structures".
3. **The Designer shows damage against hulls only.** Damage against the Constructor or a structure is shown nowhere in the game (`Hud.cpp:782-806`). The table "Hits on targets the Designer does not show" is derived from the formula and is not a display.
4. **The Designer's figures depend on the player's research.** Its ratings and figures come from the snapshot's views at the player's research, so they change as topics finish (`Hud.cpp:494-527`). The reference gives two states: no research, and every fire-rate topic done. Mixed states follow from the formulas.
5. **A splash can reach more ships in a fight than the card counts.** The card counts the ships a splash reaches in a formation. In a fight, ships pressed together can stand closer, so one hit can reach more (`DesignStats.h:109-111`; MVP §12 l.354). The real number in a fight is **Not specified**.
6. **The weapon card hides a small fire-rate upgrade.** It writes the interval to a tenth (`Hud.cpp:686-687`), so the Mass Driver's upgraded 0.3636 s still reads "0.4s".
7. **"8.8" for 8.75 rests on rounding a tie.** The figure writes 8.75 with `std::format("{:.1f}")`. `HudTests.cpp:1718` confirms "8.8" on MSVC. The figures here were rounded with Python's correctly rounded formatter; no other value in the tables is an exact tie.
8. **Two code comments point elsewhere.** `GameLogic/Matchup.h:5` cites "horizon §9", which is excluded; the in-scope source is ADR-083. `Tools/BattleModel.py:86-88` says `FORMATION_SPACING_RADII` is in `GameLogic/Simulation.cpp`, but it lives in `GameProtocol/DesignStats.h:107`. Neither changes play.
9. **The Command Station's L2 and L4 give no `guns` value.** They keep the level below's, by the `max` in `StationGuns` (`Tuning.cpp:754-763`), and the panel's line for those levels names no guns (`Hud.cpp:2658-2659`).
