# ADR-064 — A structure is upgraded a level at a time, builds the level itself, and is drawn at its level within level 1's ground

Status: **accepted** · 2026-10-25
Owns: structure-levels, structure-upgrade-command, level-hit-points, leveled-mesh-fitting, shipyard-hull-levels, station-levels-nodes-and-guns

## Context

The Command Station, the Shipyard and the Research Lab grow a level at a time (Game §structures.levels). The player orders the next level and pays for it at once, and the level builds itself over its time while the structure keeps working, with no Constructor (owner, 2026-10-04). Each level adds a share of the kind's base hit points, and a destroyed structure comes back at level 1. Every player who sees a structure sees its level, and the client draws that level's mesh (ADR-045) on level 1's ground, so that a level never changes the footprint (owner, 2026-10-04). A level's hit points add to research's, a player remembers an enemy structure at the level it last saw, and a Constructor repairs a structure being upgraded as it repairs any other (owner, 2026-10-04).

That needs an order and its refusals on the wire, a count of a level's work, a home for the levels' numbers, what the client is told of them, and a measure of level 1's size for a mesh whose shape changes from level to level. Each kind's level also gives something of its own: a Shipyard's sets the hulls it builds, a Research Lab's opens a research tier or a second slot, and a Command Station's caps the nodes its player may hold and arms the station.

## Decision

1. **The levels are tuning data** (owner, 2026-10-04). In `Tuning.json` a structure may have `levels`: the levels above the first, level 2 first, each with its `cost` in Ore and its `buildSeconds`, the time the level takes to build itself; `rules.levelHitPointsPercent` gives the hit points each level adds. The loader allows levels only on the Command Station, the Shipyard and the Research Lab, whose art has them (ADR-045), and at most up to level 5 (`MAXIMUM_STRUCTURE_LEVEL`), the art's last. A kind's top level is one more than its list's length. A Command Station's levels also carry its fleet cap ([ADR-071](ADR-071-fleet-cap.md) decision 1).
2. **`UpgradeStructureCommand` names a structure, and nothing else.** The server takes the next level's Ore when the order is given and refunds nothing. It refuses, at no cost:
   - `UnknownEntity` for an identifier that names nothing;
   - `NotUpgradable` for anything that is not one of the player's own structures;
   - `UnderConstruction` for a site still being built;
   - `AlreadyUpgrading` for a structure whose next level is under way;
   - `TopLevel` for a structure at its kind's top, which for a kind without levels is level 1;
   - `PrerequisiteMissing` for a Research Lab whose next level requires topics not researched, before its Ore is checked (decision 10);
   - `NotEnoughOre`, as a build's.

   The HUD's Upgrade button sends it. A `RepairCommand` on a structure being upgraded is taken only if the structure is damaged, as on any other; a right-click with Constructors on an undamaged one gives no repair order, and is the ordinary right-click (Game §orders.direct).
3. **A level builds itself** (owner, 2026-10-04). The structure keeps `level`, from 1, and the next level's work done and needed, in thousandths of a tick of the level's time. Each tick adds a tick, in `Simulation::BuildLevels`, so a level takes its `buildSeconds` to the tick. No Constructor works on it, and no research shortens it: the Constructors' build rate is theirs alone. The structure keeps producing, researching and firing all the while.
4. **A level lands whole.** Unlike a site's, whose hit points rise with its work, a level adds its hit points when it is in, and the structure keeps the share of them it had, as a research upgrade does (owner, 2026-10-01). The hit points are base × (research's factor + (level − 1) × `rules.levelHitPointsPercent` / 100), so a level's percent adds to research's (owner, 2026-10-04). A Constructor repairs a damaged structure while it is upgraded, as it repairs any other, and its order ends when the structure is whole.
5. **A destroyed structure takes its levels and the upgrade's Ore with it.** One built where it stood is a new structure, at level 1.
6. **The snapshot carries the level.** `EntityView` has `level`, 1 for anything that does not grow, and `upgradePermille` while a level is being built, which whoever sees the structure sees, as construction. An enemy structure out of sight is remembered as its view was last seen (ADR-024), level and all, so an upgrade made out of the player's sight shows only once it sees the structure again. `StructureTypeView` has `levels`: each level's cost, its time and the structure's full hit points at it with the player's research.
7. **The selection panel names the level and offers the next.** A structure that grows is named "Shipyard 01 · L2", and while a level is being built the panel says "Upgrading to L3, 41%". The player's own finished structure has a line for what the next level gives, "L3: 3,500 hit points", and an Upgrade button with the next level's time and cost, "Upgrade to L3 · 1:00". The button is dim while a level is under way, with the note "UPGRADING · 41%" and a bar along its foot that fills from left to right as the level builds, and dim on its cost while the Ore is short. At the top level the panel says "Top level" and has no button.
8. **Every level is drawn within level 1's ground** (owner, 2026-10-04). The client loads every level's mesh. Level 1 is fitted to the model's length along its front (ADR-018). Each later level is fitted so that the wider of its length and depth is the wider of level 1's (`Neuron::FitMeshAcross`), and so stands within level 1's square. A structure is drawn at its own level, as far as its model has levels (`DrawnLevel`), and its hardpoints and explosion are its level's. The placement ghost shows level 1. The Shipyard's art has levels 4 and 5 and the Research Lab's level 5, which the tuning data does not reach: they are loaded and never drawn, and wait for content that needs them (owner, 2026-10-25).
9. **A Shipyard's level sets the hulls it builds** (owner, 2026-10-04). The Shipyard names its hulls in the tuning data, `"hulls"` at level 1 and at each of its levels, each the hulls that level adds. The loader allows `hulls` on a Shipyard only, and a Shipyard that names any names every hull exactly once; one that names none builds every hull at level 1. Each `HullView` carries `shipyardLevel`.
   - **The server refuses a job above the Shipyard's level** with `LevelTooLow`. A job already queued is never taken out, since a standing Shipyard's level only rises. The Large Hull topic decides whether a Large design can be saved; the Shipyard's level decides where it is built.
   - **The client says why.** The designer's Queue is dim for a design above the target Shipyard's level, and its line reads "Needs Shipyard L2" in place of the build time; the design is still saved. The production window dims such a design, its code followed by "NEEDS L2". A Shipyard's panel names the hulls its next level adds and its hit points.
   - How the AI plays the levels is [ADR-020](ADR-020-ai-and-match-flow.md) decision 14's.
10. **A Research Lab's level opens a research tier, and a later one gives a second slot** (owner, 2026-10-04). The Lab's levels in `Tuning.json` carry `"opensTier"`, `"requires"` and `"researchSlots"`, which only a Lab's level may have: the tier a level opens, the topics its upgrade requires, and the topics it researches at once. What a tier is, and the loader's tier checks, are [ADR-033](ADR-033-research-tiers.md) decision 2's, and how the second slot runs is [ADR-017](ADR-017-research-and-the-designer.md) decision 1's.
    - **The upgrade waits for its research.** A Lab whose next level requires topics not researched is refused with `PrerequisiteMissing`, before its Ore is checked.
    - **The snapshot** carries each level's tier, slots and required topics in `StructureLevelView`, and the second topic's progress; the player's open tier is [ADR-033](ADR-033-research-tiers.md) decision 5's.
    - **The client.** The research window shows a second running topic's progress, and dims a topic of a tier not open as ADR-033 decision 5 has it. The Lab's panel names what its next level gives, its tier or its second slot and its hit points, and the topics it still needs, "L2 needs Improved Extraction", its button dim with NEEDS RESEARCH until they are researched. The match log writes a tier when the player's open tier rises ([ADR-038](ADR-038-phase-one-match-log.md)).
    - How the AI researches and upgrades its Lab, and holds production back for an upgrade it cannot yet pay for, is [ADR-020](ADR-020-ai-and-match-flow.md) decision 14's.
11. **A Command Station's level caps the nodes its player may hold, and gives it its Defence guns** (owner, 2026-10-04; the caps, 2026-10-06). The station carries `"nodes"` and `"guns"` in `Tuning.json`, and so may each of its levels; a level without `guns` keeps the level below's. The loader allows `nodes` and `guns` on a Command Station only, and a gun count of at least 1.
    - **The cap is [ADR-056](ADR-056-territory.md) decision 4's**, which refuses a Relay past it. The snapshot carries the player's cap in `nodeCap`, zero on a map without territory, and each level's `nodes` and `guns` in `StructureLevelView`.
    - **Each gun is the station's gun, on a rhythm of its own.** Each gun after the first keeps its own reload, so the guns do not fire together, and fires at the target the station holds, chosen by the same rule. With the nearest-target rule every gun shoots at the same enemy, which is what "aiming on its own" comes to when each aims by one rule; under another target rule each shot chooses its own, as every shot does. A shot names its gun, `ShotView::gun`, from 0.
    - **The client spreads the guns over the model.** A shot of gun *n* leaves from the *n*-th nearest of the station's gun hardpoints to its target, wrapping round, so that every gun hardpoint fires (`NearestMuzzle`). The station's panel names what its next level gives: its nodes, its Defence guns, its fleet cap ([ADR-071](ADR-071-fleet-cap.md)) and its hit points, "L3: 10 nodes, 2 Defence guns, fleet 30, 7,000 hit points".
    - How the AI orders the station's levels is [ADR-020](ADR-020-ai-and-match-flow.md) decision 14's.

## Consequences

- **Why the width and not the length.** Fitted by its length alone, a level whose depth grows more than its length overhangs its footprint. Fitted by the wider of its length and depth, every level stands within level 1's square, and a later level reads as more detail, not more size.
- **Every level's mesh loads at startup.** Both sets' three growing models load each of their levels, in the parallel startup of ADR-049.
- **A Medium hull waits for a Shipyard at the level that adds it.**
- **Territory is capped by the station's level.** A player claims past its cap only by upgrading its Command Station.
- **Upgrades cost no Constructor time.** A player gets a level for its Ore and its wait alone, and its Constructors stay free for sites and repairs.
- **Tests.** `UpgradeTests` covers the order and every refusal, the payment, a level building itself in its time with no work for a Constructor, a climb to the top level, a Shipyard building during its upgrade, a damaged structure keeping its share and being repaired during its upgrade, a level's percent adding to research's, a structure destroyed during an upgrade and rebuilt at level 1, the snapshot's levels and progress, and an enemy's level remembered under fog. `ProductionTests.BuildsTheHullsOfItsLevel` covers each hull at each level, `ResearchTests.ALabsLevelOpensItsTier` and `ALevelFourLabResearchesTwoAtOnce` the Lab's levels, `TuningTests` the levels' and hulls' loading and refusals, `WireFormatTests` the levels' fields and the command. `HudTests.NamesALevelAndOffersTheNext` covers the panel and `DrawsAButtonsWorkAsABar` the upgrade's bar, `HudTests.SaysWhatAShipyardCannotBuild` the designer, the production window and the hulls a level adds, `HudTests.ShowsTheLabsLevels` and `ListsTheTopicsTierByTier` the Lab's, `TerritoryTests.TheStationCapsTheNodesHeld` the cap at levels 1 and 2, a site counting, and level 1's cap with no node taken away once the station is gone, `DefenseTests.AStationsLevelGivesItsGuns` the guns at each level, `HudTests.ShowsTheStationsCap` the station's next level, `HardpointsTests.AFurtherGunFiresFromTheNextNearest` and `CombatEffectsTests.TheViewIsToldWhichGunFired` the guns' muzzles, and `ModelCatalogTests.FitsEveryLevelOnTheFirstLevelsGround` the fitting of every repository level and `DrawnLevel`.

## What this forecloses

- A level that changes a structure's footprint, its placement or its pathing, without a new decision.
- Cancelling an upgrade, or a refund when one is lost, as with every other job (ADR-016).
- Levels for the Relay, the Defence Platform or the Mining Rig, until their art has them.
- Skipping a level: each is ordered and built on its own.
