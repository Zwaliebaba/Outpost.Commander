# ADR-088 — Selection and commands: what the pointer is over, the command card, and the bars on the screen

Status: **accepted** · 2026-10-25
Owns: hud-hover, selection-order-buttons, command-card, build-mode, retreat-choice-row, selection-title, selection-ship-bars, entity-bars, health-bar-colors

## Context

The selection and its commands are what the player reads and reaches for most, and four things make them hard to read or reach unless they are laid out for it (owner, 2026-10-08):

- **A control has to say it takes a click before it is clicked.** A HUD that does not answer the pointer leaves the player to learn which text is a button.
- **The commands need a place of their own that never moves.** A warship's orders need buttons as well as keys, and a button the hand learns has to stand where it stood. Beside a selection panel as wide as its longest line, centered as a pair, the commands moved by up to 140 units as the selection changed, and the two panels' tops stood apart. The bottom of the screen is one console, as StarCraft II's is: the minimap in the bottom-left corner, the selection in the middle and a command card in the bottom-right corner, each command in a cell it always takes (owner, 2026-10-28).
- **The selection has to say its count once and show who is hurt.** A count repeated over a line that already holds it says nothing, and one bar for a whole group hides the ship that is about to be lost.
- **A bar drawn on the ground turns and foreshortens with the camera.** A turn stands it on end or puts it under its unit, and the pitch shortens it. A bar has to read the same at every turn, pitch and zoom.

The retreat is a choice of three, which the player should see whole and pick in one press. A Constructor's six structures, with its retreat, would make a column of buttons that covers the bottom of the screen where the ships the player is working with stand, and in a window of their own they would outlive the Constructor that builds them; they take the card's place while the player asks for them, as StarCraft II's build menu does (owner, 2026-10-28).

## Decision

1. **The HUD answers the pointer.** `Hud::Lay` takes the action under the pointer, as `Hud::Describe` does, which `GameClient` finds in the last frame's layout (`Layout::HoverAt`): an enabled button, or a locked part's card, which previews and takes no click ([ADR-017](ADR-017-research-and-the-designer.md) decision 7). A click takes only a button (`Layout::ActionAt`).
   - An enabled button that takes it has its edge lit in the windows' edge color, (0.25, 0.43, 0.76), short of a pick's edge. A pick keeps its own.
   - A status line that takes it lies on the cards' color. Lines whose click does the same light together, since a click on either does what a click on the other would: the Shipyards' line and the fleet's both open production.
   - The pointer changes nothing else: text the player cannot click, and every word, place and click, are the same with the pointer or without it, and `Lay` without a hovered action lays out the same.
2. **The commands stand on the command card,** in the bottom-right corner, and the selection in the middle of the bottom edge, on one line with the minimap (owner, 2026-10-28).
   - **The card** stands 16 units in from the screen's right edge and its foot, 380 by 209 units in every selection and mode, so that nothing on it moves as the selection changes. Inside its padding of 12 it has two columns of cells, each 175 units wide with 6 between, in three rows of a button's 34 units, and at its foot the retreat (decision 3). Each button takes the cell it always takes (`Hud::Button::cell`), or the whole of its row (`wide`); a cell no button takes stands empty on the card's face. It is not drawn while the selection offers no button, no retreat and no build mode, as for another player's structure.

     | Row | Left cell | Right cell |
     |---|---|---|
     | 1 | Attack-move; or a structure's first window, Production, or Research on a Research Lab | Hold sector; or the Shipyard's Ship designer |
     | 2 | Patrol | Stop |
     | 3 | Build | — |
     | 3, a structure that grows | Upgrade, across the row ([ADR-064](ADR-064-structure-upgrades.md) decision 7) | |

     A structure is selected alone and ships never with one, so the rows hold either a selection of ships' cells or a structure's. 209 units is build mode's height: its padding, its label's line of 20 units and three rows of structures of 51 units 6 apart; three rows of buttons and the retreat under them, 198 units, fit within it, the 11 to spare over the retreat.
   - **The selection panel** stands in the middle of the screen, moved only as far as keeps it 8 units clear of the minimap and of the card's place, whether or not the card is drawn. It is as wide as its longest line, from 280 units to 552, the room a 16:9 screen at 150% leaves it between the two, and as tall as what it holds. Its foot, the card's and the minimap's stand on the bottom margin's line. The bottom row is then 16 + 300 + 8 + 552 + 8 + 380 + 16 = 1,280 units at its widest, as wide as a 16:9 screen at 150% ([ADR-070](ADR-070-interface-scale.md) decision 3).
   - **A selection with a warship offers its orders:** Attack-move, Hold sector, Patrol and Stop, each with its key's cap from `KeyBindings.h`, A, H, T and S, in the card's first two rows. Hold sector is offered only on a map with sectors, as the Relay is ([ADR-056](ADR-056-territory.md)), and its cell stands empty without them. A selection of Constructors alone offers none.
   - **A selection that holds a Constructor offers one Build button,** a mixed one included, with B's cap (`KEY_BUILD`), in the left cell of the last row. It and B turn the card's build mode on, and off again (`PlayerControls::ToggleBuilding`, `ActionKind::OpenBuild`); B does nothing without a Constructor. In build mode the card holds BUILD with B's cap at its right, over a cell for each structure the Constructors build (`Hud::DescribeBuild`), and neither its buttons nor the retreat. A structure's cell is its place among the structures the tuning data lets Constructors build, so that a map without sectors leaves the Relay's empty; the card has six, and a seventh structure would need a cell decided with it. Each cell is a button on two lines (`Neuron::ButtonContent::stacked`), the structure's name over its cost, or over the reason it cannot be built, ONE PER PLAYER or NODE CAP, dim when the Ore is short ([ADR-043](ADR-043-hud-in-the-windows-look.md) decision 4).
   - **Build mode** lasts while the selection holds a Constructor, and a Constructor selected again after one without shows the card's buttons. Picking a structure arms its placement and leaves build mode on, so that another can be picked once it is placed; Shift keeps the placement armed. Esc cancels an armed placement first, then turns build mode off, before it closes a window ([ADR-031](ADR-031-floating-windows.md) decision 5), and a right-click cancels a placement and leaves build mode on. The orders' keys give them while their buttons are not shown. No structure has a key of its own.
   - **A button does what its key does.** `PlayerControls` has `ArmAttackMove`, `ArmStanding` and `Stop`, which the keys and the buttons both call, and four action kinds name them. Attack-move, Hold sector and Patrol arm the next left-click, each in place of what was armed; Stop orders at once. They act on every selected ship, as the keys do.
   - **The armed one is lit,** drawn as a pick, until it is given or canceled: `Hud::Describe` takes the order the controls have armed. An order is given on the left press, and a release only acts on a press made in the world, so the press on a button never gives the order it arms.
3. **The retreat is three choices,** so that the player sees every choice and picks one in a press.
   - **The selection's** stands at the command card's foot, under its cells and not among them (`Hud::Content::retreat`): RETREAT over three cells, "25%", "50%" and "Never", each a button's height and a third of its width. The first ship's setting is lit as a pick, or, while the ships differ, none is and MIXED stands at the label's right. A press sets the cell's for every selected ship (`ActionKind::SetRetreat`), and a cell under the pointer has its edge lit (decision 1).
   - **The designer's** stands in a row of 206 by 48 units between Rename and the ship count: RETREAT in its labels' face over three cells of 66 by 30 units, "25%", "50%" and "NEVER". A press sets the design's retreat (`ActionKind::DesignRetreat`, `Designer::SetRetreat`). The whole-HUD overlap and contrast tests find room for it.
4. **The selection says its count once and shows each ship.**
   - **Ships of one design are titled once,** "7 × Lancer+SA", with the multiplication sign; two Constructors read "2 × Constructor". Ships of several designs have "12 ships" over a line for each design, "3 × Swarm", the most numerous first, at most five lines and "and 2 more designs" for the rest. One ship has its design's name.
   - **The selection writes its hit points in figures too,** "Hit points 3,599 / 4,095", in whole points as [ADR-014](ADR-014-designs-and-combat.md) decision 3 has them.
   - **Two to 24 ships have a bar each** under the lines (`Hud::Content::shipBars`, `Hud::SHIP_BARS_MOST`). The bars stand in rows of twelve, each 17 by 10 units with 4 between, design by design in the lines' order and, within a design, in the selection's. Twelve fit the narrowest selection panel: 248 units of the 256 inside its padding, which a `static_assert` holds.
   - **Each fills from the left by its ship's share of hit points,** green, amber or red as every health bar does (decision 5). A retreating ship's has a line round it, outside it, in the text's color; otherwise the bar under the pointer has one in the edge's (decision 1).
   - **A click on a bar selects that ship alone** and centers the camera on it, as a click on the Shipyards' line at the cap does for the Command Station (`ActionKind::Select`, [ADR-066](ADR-066-production-status-on-the-hud.md)).
   - **One ship has the selection's one bar,** since a bar of its own would be the same bar, narrower, and so does a selection of more than 24.
   - A module on a starting design's hull, drive and weapon is named for that design, as [ADR-069](ADR-069-starting-design-names.md) decision 5 has it.
5. **The bars over an entity are on the screen.**
   - **`GameClient` places them and the HUD lays them out,** so `Hud::Lay` holds no camera ([ADR-015](ADR-015-ui-drawing.md)). `Camera::PixelAbove` gives their foot: in the entity's column, level with the highest on the screen of the eight corners of its model's bounds, placed by the model's pose, tilt and stance included (`GameClient::BoundsCorners`). An entity without a model gives the corners of its footprint's square on the ground. The foot is above the model whatever its size, the zoom and the camera's turn (owner, 2026-10-10).
   - **What shows a bar** is `Hud::BarOver`'s: the health bar while the entity is hurt, and over every ship and structure, whole or not, while Alt is held (`KEY_EVERY_HEALTH_BAR`); the build bar while it is unfinished; and nothing for what the player only remembers ([ADR-081](ADR-081-territory-and-memory-over-the-fog.md) decision 4). `Hud::Content::entityBars` carries each one's foot, shares and back.
   - **A bar is 32 by 5 reference units at the back buffer's scale, at every zoom.** The player's factor ([ADR-070](ADR-070-interface-scale.md)) does not scale it, since the factor sizes the HUD and not the world's marks. Its place and size are whole pixels, so that it keeps its size as it moves.
   - **It fills from the left however the camera has turned.** The health bar stands over the build bar, 2 units apart, the pair 4 units above the foot and centered over it. Each is a back of its side's color at 0.3, dark gray for a side the data does not name, so that a bar says whose it is, with its fill from the left: the health green, amber or red, and the build gray ([ADR-085](ADR-085-one-meaning-per-color.md) decision 2).
   - **Every health bar fills from one set of colors** (owner, 2026-10-10): the bars over an entity, the selection's ships' bars and its one bar, (0.2, 0.85, 0.3) above half its hit points, (1.0, 0.7, 0.1) above a quarter and (0.95, 0.2, 0.15) below, linear.
     - The figures' set is tuned to a window's navy ([ADR-062](ADR-062-type-scale-and-contrast.md) decision 3). On a side's back at 0.3 its red would stand at 1.67:1 and its amber at 3.32:1. This set's stand at 2.36:1 and 4.47:1 or more.
     - Those ratios are WCAG's, of the linear colors, against the backs of `Models.json`'s three sides' colors and of the gray a side the data does not name gets. They are computed, not measured on screen.
     - The figures, the designer's changes and the production cards' ratings keep the figures' set.
   - **The bars are drawn first, under every panel and window,** from a list of their own (`Hud::Layout::bars`) that takes no click and covers nothing (`Layout::Covers`), so that a click on a bar is the world's. A pair wholly off the screen is left out.

## Consequences

- **What lights is the HUD's own, the orders window's Give and the leave window's two buttons,** which are drawn as HUD buttons (owner, 2026-10-25). The windows' cards, whose numbers the designer previews under the pointer, and the menu's buttons do not light, and nothing has a pressed state.
- **A press picks a retreat; nothing steps one.**
- **The commands are in one place whatever is selected,** at the price of eye travel: from the middle of a 280-unit selection to the middle of the card is 754 units at 100% on a 16:9 screen, and 1,074 on a 2560×1080 one, computed from the places above. A lone Constructor's card holds Build and the retreat in a frame of 209 units, two rows empty.
- **The designer at its tallest covers the card's top two rows** at 100% on a 16:9 screen until the player moves it: 938 units from the top margin, its foot at 954, 99 below the card's top at 855. A window stands over the console as it stands over the rest of the HUD (owner, 2026-10-28).
- **A bar costs two of the interface's quads, and a pair four,** of `UiPipeline::MAX_QUADS` a frame.
- **Tests of decision 1:** `HudTests.LightsWhatThePointerIsOver` checks nothing lit without a pointer; the Research button's four edges lit, and only its, with the status line that opens research on the cards' color; over production, the enabled button and not a dim one naming the same action, and both lines that open it; and every text, its place and every click the same as without the pointer. `HudTests.LaysOutTheOrdersWindow` checks Give's four edges lit under the pointer and none without it.
- **Tests of decision 2:**
  - `HudTests.OffersTheOrdersOnTheCommandCard` checks the four orders with their caps in the first two rows, Hold sector only with sectors, none for Constructors alone, the armed one lit, and for three widths of the selection panel at 1920 and 1280 wide, the card in the corner, 209 units tall, and the selection in the middle on its line. `LaysOutTheCommandCardInCells` checks each button's cell, a wide one across its row, an empty cell taking no press, the retreat at the foot, a lone Constructor's card as tall, and the minimap's, the selection's and the card's feet on one line; `FitsTheSelectionPanelToItsLines` the selection's widths, its middle, and its widest at 150% against the minimap's gap; `LaysOutButtonsForClicks` finds a button in the card's first cell with no selection. `PlayerControlsTests.GivesTheOrdersOfTheHudsButtons` checks each call arming or ordering as its key does, each replacing the last, and none without a selection. `PlayerControlsTests` and `GameClient` need DirectXMath, so CI runs them.
  - `HudTests.OffersOneBuildButtonForBuildMode` checks a warship and a Constructor offering the orders and Build with B's cap in their cells, and no structure, with the retreat, Build laid out as a button round its word that a press takes, and no Build without a Constructor; `OffersTheStructuresToConstructors` a Constructor alone offering Build in its cell and its retreat, and the structures in their cells; `OffersTheRelayOnlyWithSectors` the Relay's cell, and no more structures than cells. `LaysOutTheCommandCardInBuildMode` checks, at 100% and 150%, the card in the corner as tall, BUILD and B's cap, neither the buttons nor the retreat, a structure's place and press, none for one that cannot be built, and its reason under its name. `CanvasTests.StacksAButtonsFigureUnderItsLabel` checks the two lines. `PlayerControlsTests.TurnsBuildModeOnAndOffForConstructors` checks B and the Build button, nothing without a Constructor, placing leaving it on, Esc's order, and a selection without a Constructor ending it for good.
- **Tests of decision 3:** `HudTests.SetsTheSelectionsRetreat` checks the row's setting, MIXED and none lit for ships that differ, a press for each cell, a lone ship's own setting lit, and no row without a ship; `SetsTheDesignsRetreatInARow` the designer's three cells and their presses; the designer's panel test the setting a pick changes and its UPDATE; `DesignerTests.SetsTheDesignsRetreat` `SetRetreat`. The tests that count a selection's buttons do not count the retreat among them.
- **Tests of decision 4:** `HudTests.GivesEachShipABar` checks seven ships of one design titled once; their bars at 1080 and 2160 lines, one row in the ships' order, each 17 by 10 units under the lines and inside the panel, a click on each selecting its ship; a hurt ship's fill short and red, a whole one's full and green, and a retreating ship's four lines in the text's color; the bar under the pointer lit with four lines in the edge's color and nothing else; 24 ships of two designs in two rows of twelve; and 25 falling back to the one bar. `DescribesOneShip` and `DescribesAGroupByDesign` check the × sign, the hit points in figures and the bars' order. `DrawsTheSelectionsHealthAsABar` checks the one bar under the lines, its panel anchored to the bottom, filled by the share of hit points and red below a quarter. `LongestContent` selects 24 ships, four of each of its six designs, so the whole-HUD overlap and contrast tests see both rows. `DesignerTests.NamesAModuleAfterItsStartingDesign` checks "Lancer+SA", its save, a typed name kept, no saved design to follow, a saved one named for its components, and a name too long with the initials; `PicksAModuleOrNone` finds "Swarm+SA".
- **Tests of decision 5:** `HudTests.LaysTheBarsOverTheEntities` checks, at 1080 and 2160 lines and at factors 1 and 1.5, each bar 32 by 5 units at the back buffer's scale, centered over its foot and 4 units above it, the health over the build 2 units apart, each fill from its back's left at its share in gray, amber or green, the side's back, none off the screen, no click and no cover on a bar, and the selection panel covering one under it. `PutsABarOnlyOverWhatShowsOne` checks `BarOver` for a whole ship, Alt, a hurt one, an unfinished structure whole and hurt, a memory of each, and an asteroid. `CameraTests.PlacesABarAboveItsModelAtEveryTurn` checks the foot in its entity's column, level with the highest of a turned, lifted box's corners on the screen and above 36 points of its footprint's rim, with the camera turned 0°, 90°, 180° and 270° at three zooms, and the bar laid there, 4 units above, filling from the left. The camera's test and `GameClient` need DirectXMath and D3D12, so CI runs them.

## What this forecloses

- A hover state the player must learn: what lights is what a click takes.
- **Dropping the selection once it is given an order** (owner, 2026-10-25), for warships and Constructors alike. A fight is a run of orders to the same ships, and the selection is what S, the retreat and the orders window act on.
- **A bar whose length tells its entity's size,** short of a new decision: every bar is 32 units long.
- A health bar smaller on screen than 32 × 5 reference units.
